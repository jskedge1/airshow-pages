# Campaign: Plan your 2027 airshow season

**Site:** The Big Guide to UK Airshows (UK Airshow Guide), ukairshowguide.co.uk
**Window:** late November 2026 to early May 2027 (the season opens Sat 8 May 2027, IWM Duxford)
**Hero asset:** a 20 s motion video in three shapes (below)

## The idea

Winter is when people plan their airshow summer: dates get confirmed, tickets go on sale and
travel and camping get booked. The campaign makes the guide the place where that planning
starts. One message, in the site's own words:

> **Every UK airshow, one easy guide. Plan your 2027 airshow, stop by stop.**

Proof points, all taken from the live pages (re-check them before each post, since the calendar updates every week):
- **46 shows confirmed** for 2027 so far, 22 still to come (calendar, last updated 5 Nov 2026)
- Season opens **Sat 8 May, IWM Duxford** and ends **Sat 2 October, Duxford**
- **13 featured shows**, each with its own guide; the airshow map finds the nearest ones
- **54 bus routes mapped** and 12 train route pages: "Leave the car at home"
- Camping hub and Photography hub (settings by aircraft, kit calculator)

## Audience

1. **Regular airshow-goers** planning several shows a year. Hook: the whole season in one place, dates as they're confirmed.
2. **Families and first-timers.** Hook: "Planning your first airshow", what to pack, getting there without a car.
3. **Aviation photographers.** Hook: Photography hub, settings by aircraft, kit calculator.
4. **Campers.** Hook: Camping hub, "From your first tent to a full base camp".
