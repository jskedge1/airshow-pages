Before you write

Quick answers

A few things people often ask. If your question is about a particular show on the day, the organiser's own website and social media will always have the latest word.

Can you get me tickets or a refund?

I'm afraid not. This guide is independent and isn't connected to any show, so I can't sell, change or refund tickets. Every show page has a link to the official ticket site. I would advise booking direct with the organiser, as resold tickets are often refused at the gate.

I lost something at a show

I would contact the show organiser as soon as you can. Most shows run a lost property service for a week or two afterwards, and some hand items to the local police. The show page's Useful links section will point you to the organiser.

When will the flying times and line-up be out?

Most shows publish flying times only a day or two before, and line-ups change right up to the weekend. I update the show pages as soon as anything is confirmed. Where times aren't published yet, I mark them as approx. so you can still plan.

Can I use your photos?

All photos on the site are my own. Please don't copy them without asking. If you'd like to use one for a club newsletter, a print or anything else, choose "Photos and media" above and tell me which image and what it's for.

I run a show. Can you list it?

Yes please. Choose "Suggest a show" above and include the dates, the venue and a link to your website. Press packs, site maps and confirmed line-ups help me write a fuller page.

Do you take advertising?

I'm open to working with businesses that genuinely help people enjoy air shows, such as nearby places to stay, camera hire or transport. Choose "Working together" above and tell me a little about what you have in mind.

Spotted a change?

Shows change parking, gate times and bus routes from year to year, and I can't be at every briefing. If you've been and something was different on the day, tell me what you found. It helps the next person plan a better day out.
