I built My Airshow Hub because my own planning used to live in five different places. Screenshots, notes on my phone, emails with train tickets. The hub keeps every show you are going to in one place, and it stays with you right up to the crowd line.

This page walks you through it. How you join, what you get, and how it helps from the day you book to the drive home.

Joining Takes About A Minute

Four steps and you are in. I have shown each one with the real screens, filled in with an example member called Alex.

1

Open The Hub

Tap Airshow Hub in the menu at the top of any page. It opens on the hub's welcome screen, with a Log In tab and a Join free tab.

If you have used the forum before, you already have an account. Just log in.

You do this
2

Pick How You Sign In

A secure members window opens. Use your email and a password, or carry on with Google or Facebook if that is easier.

This window is run by Wix, who host the site, so I never see your password.

Handled by Wix
I would suggest using the same sign in every time. If you join with Facebook and later try your email, it can look like you have two accounts.
3

Save Your First Show

Go to My Trips and choose a show from the list. That's it. It gets its own card, a countdown and a simple tick sheet for tickets, trains and parking.

Going to more than one this year? Add them all. Alex has RIAT and Eastbourne saved here.

You do this
4

Your Hub Is Ready

Your next show now sits at the top of the hub with a countdown to the gates opening. Underneath, the trip readiness dial levels out as you tick things off.

Next time, you just log in and it is all there, on any device.

Done for you
I would advise having a look round every tab once you are in. It only takes a couple of minutes, and you might find a tool you did not know you needed.

How It Helps Through The Season

Pick a stage below. The hub changes what it shows you as your show gets closer, so it is just as useful in March as it is on the crowd line.

Before The Show

This is where most of the hub's work happens. Months out, it keeps an eye on things so you don't have to.

Saved shows and countdownsSave any featured show and its countdown sits on your hub. When the show changes its details, you see it there first.
Must see aircraft alertsTap "Add to must see" on any aircraft in a show's line up. If it gets confirmed or cancelled, the hub tells you.
Trip tick sheetTickets booked, need printing, trains sorted, parking paid. A simple list for each show, with nothing to upload.
Budget trackerTickets, travel, food and camping in one running total. No nasty surprises when you get to the food stalls.
Group planner and packing listShare a plan with the people you are going with, using a code only they get. The packing list changes for a day visitor, a photographer or a camper.

On Show Day

Leave the hub open on your phone. This is the bit I use most once I am through the gate.

Show day modeUpload the flying display page from your programme and it shows what is flying now and next. A good one for planning a toilet break.
Live inbound radarWatch display aircraft heading in to the show, so the camera is ready before they arrive.
Show weather panelOpens about a week before. Wind, cloud and rain for the show site, so you pack the right kit. Unfortunately the weather on the day will be what it will be, so plan accordingly.
Ear defender alertsA heads up before the loudest jets display. Your child's ears are delicate, so try to protect them as much as possible.

Good To Know

The questions I get asked most about the hub. If yours isn't here, drop me a message.

Ask me something →
Is it really free?

Yes. Every tool in the hub and the forum is free, and you are never asked for card details. The guide earns a little through links and partners instead, so it can stay free for visitors.

Do I need a Facebook account?

No. Facebook and Google are just quicker ways in. An email address and a password works exactly the same.

Will it work on my phone?

It was built with the phone in mind, as that is what you will have in your hand on show day. You can also add the site to your home screen, so it opens like an app.

What if a show changes its line up?

When a show's official website updates, the guide picks it up and the change shows in your Updates tab. If the aircraft is on your must see list, you get told about it straight away.

I would still advise checking the official show website before you travel, as things can change late on.

I already joined the forum. Do I need a new account?

No. The hub and the forum use the same log in, so just log in and your hub is waiting for you.

Can I delete my account?

Of course. Email me at contactus@ukairshowguide.co.uk and I will remove it for you.
