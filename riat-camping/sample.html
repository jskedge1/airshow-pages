Getting To The Show

How to get from your pitch to the showground at MOD Boscombe Down. RIAT's own travel plan for 2027 is still to be announced, so I would check back nearer the show.

01

Driving

About 10 minutes

02

Shuttle

Our own minibus to the show car park, every 30 minutes on show days. £5 return.

03

Walking

No safe walking route, so please drive or use the shuttle.

04

Taxi

Local taxi firms will pick up from the gate. Book early for the evening.

05

Your car

Your car stays on your pitch all week.

Show week is busy, so these are the things I would ask any campsite before paying.

1

Your datesWhen you can arrive and leave, and whether there is a minimum stay.

2

What the price includesHow many people and cars, and whether electric costs extra.

3

The cancellation policyRIAT's travel plan is still to come, so I would look for a booking you can change.

4

Getting to the showHow long the drive is, and whether the campsite runs its own shuttle.

The site rulesBarbecues, campfires, generators, dogs and quiet hours.
