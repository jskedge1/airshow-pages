Getting to the show

How to get from your pitch to the showground at MOD Boscombe Down. RIAT's own travel plan for 2027 is still to be announced.

Driving

About 10 minutes

Shuttle

Our own minibus to the show car park, every 30 minutes on show days. £5 return.

Walking

No safe walking route, so please drive or use the shuttle.

Taxi

Local taxi firms will pick up from the gate. Book early for the evening.

Your car

Your car stays on your pitch all week.

Good to know

Site rules and the things visitors usually ask.

Quiet time
Please keep it down from 23:00 to 07:00.
Families
Children welcome. There is a small play area by the farm gate.
Groups
Groups of up to 4 pitches can be booked together. Bigger groups, please ask.
Accessibility
Level grass, with an accessible toilet near the entrance. No accessible shower yet.
Arrivals
You can see the arrivals on Wednesday and Thursday from the top of the field.

Book

You book directly with the campsite. The guide does not take bookings or payments.
