Meet Pip your airshow guide

A first look at a mascot for The Big Guide to UK Airshows: a small round propeller plane in the site's own blue, teal and red, who turns up wherever a visitor needs a tip, a warning or a bit of encouragement.

  • Wings work as arms, so Pip can wave, point, hold a map or raise a marshal's paddles.
  • One drawing, many poses. Every pose on this page comes from a single small file, so new ones take minutes.
  • Reads at any size, from a forum avatar to a full page header.
  • "Pip" is a working name. It's yours to rename.
