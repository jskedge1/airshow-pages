# Pip Hint Maker

Hide a little piece of Pip in a normal airshow photo before Pip's reveal (see `mascot/launch.html`).

- Use it here: https://claude.ai/artifact/XSMYUP4MUMoYgQXXmMqNR4 (saves the photo to your device; the photo never leaves it).
- The hint stickers come from `mascot/hints/build.js`, which also makes the seven hint posts in `mascot/hints/posts/`.
- `assets/sample.jpg` is Jack's own A400M photo from `youtube-img/`.
