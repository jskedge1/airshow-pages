What Pip is

Pip is a small, round propeller plane in the guide's own blue, teal and red. Pip isn't any real aircraft type and never flies in the display. Pip watches from the crowd line, just like you.

For children, Pip is a friend who makes a big, loud day feel manageable. Pip wears ear defenders when it gets noisy, which is exactly the habit I want kids to copy.

Pip in four words

Curious

Pip loves finding things out and wants to know what every aircraft is.

Helpful

Pip points the way, holds the map and waves the marshal's paddles.

Calm

Queues, rain and crowds don't bother Pip, so they needn't bother you.

Cheerful

Pip is pleased to see you and happy when your day goes well.

Who Pip is for

Pip is for anyone who would like a friendly face while they sort the day out. Mostly, that is three groups of people.

Most of all

Families with young children

Pip makes a long, loud day feel friendly, and gives kids something to do.

New to airshows

First timers

Pip's Plan, Inform and Prepare series walk you through your first show, one step at a time.

A lighter touch

Regulars and enthusiasts

You come for the detail, so Pip stays in the corner and the facts stay up front.

Pip and Jack

I'm Jack, and I write this guide. Pip is the friendly face. We each have our own job.

What I do

  • Write every tip, opinion and recommendation
  • Check every fact, date, time and price
  • Write the reviews, scores and comparisons
  • Keep the Jack's tip boxes as my own

What Pip does

  • Points the way and shows whether it's Plan, Inform or Prepare
  • Stars in the cartoons, trailers and social graphics
  • Runs the kids' missions and spotter cards
  • Cheers, waves and keeps you company while a page loads

The golden rule. Pip never says a fact or an opinion that I haven't written first.

How Pip talks

Short, warm lines that a seven year old can read on their own. Pip never shouts and never sells.

  • Got your ear defenders? Little ears need looking after.
  • Let's plan your day. Which show are we going to?
  • You spotted them all. That's a proper spotter's eye!
  • A bit of rain? Pack a coat and we're still on.
