What Pip is

Pip is a small, round propeller plane. Pip isn't any real aircraft type and never flies in the display. Pip watches from the crowd line, just like you.

When your child spots Pip, they know it must be airshow season. Pip is the sign that the fun is about to start.

For children, Pip is a friend who makes a big, loud day feel manageable. Pip wears ear defenders when it gets noisy, which is exactly the habit I want kids to copy.

  • CuriousPip loves finding things out and wants to know what every aircraft is.
  • HelpfulPip points the way, holds the map and waves the marshal's paddles.
  • CalmQueues, rain and crowds don't bother Pip, so they needn't bother you.
  • CheerfulPip is pleased to see you and happy when your day goes well.

Who Pip is for

Pip is for anyone who would like a friendly face while they sort the day out. Mostly, that is three groups of people.

  1. 01

The friendly face

What Pip does

Pip is the sidekick, and we each have our own job.

  • Points the way and shows whether it's Plan, Inform or Prepare
  • Stars in the cartoons, trailers and social graphics
  • Runs the kids' missions and spotter cards
  • Cheers, waves and keeps you company while a page loads
The golden rulePip never says a fact or an opinion that I haven't written first.

How Pip talks

Short, warm lines that a seven year old can read on their own. Pip never shouts and never sells.

  • Got your ear defenders? Little ears need looking after.
  • Let's plan your day. Which show are we going to?
  • You spotted them all. That's a proper spotter's eye!
  • A bit of rain? Pack a coat and we're still on.

Where you'll see Pip

Pip is for the cartoons and graphics, not for everything. That way Pip stays special, and you notice when Pip turns up.

You won't see Pip on serious news such as cancellations and remembrance, or on security rules, medical and accessibility information. Those are the places where plain words matter most, so Pip steps aside.
