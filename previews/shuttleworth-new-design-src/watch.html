Where to watch, on the map

These are the spots I would pick at Old Warden, each one pinned on Google Maps so you can see where it sits along the crowd line. Pick a tab to see who it suits and the good and bad points.

Show centre, northern paddocks

Inside the show, on the fence opposite show centre

If I could only stand in one place at Old Warden, it would be here. You’re on the fence opposite show centre, and as the aircraft fly round the curve of the crowd line you get lovely topside passes.

It works for both display lines. It fills first, so I’d arrive early and save your pitch.

  • SuitsEnthusiasts and photographers
  • LightThe sun moves behind you through the afternoon
  • Watch outThe busiest part of the fence

Top of Paddock 1, by the hangars

Inside the show, at the top of Paddock 1, in front of the museum hangars

Set back on the slope in front of the museum hangars, you look out over the heads of the crowd, so you don’t need a front row spot.

This might work for your family if you arrive later in the morning, as the toilets, Runway Café and hangars are all on your doorstep.

  • SuitsLate arrivals and young children
  • FacilitiesHangars, toilets and café right behind
  • Watch outFurther from the action on the display line

The middle paddocks

Inside the show, around the middle of the crowd line

At the biggest shows, when the crowd spreads down into Paddocks 4 and 5, show centre moves down to around here.

On the busy days this is a good place to try if the northern fence is already full, and you still get a straight view across the runway.

  • SuitsBig show days like the Festival Of Flight
  • LightSun behind you later in the day
  • Watch outFills quickly on the busiest days

Southern paddocks, 4 and 5

Inside the show, in the southern paddocks, closest to the Alder Drive entrance

This is the quietest part of the showground at the smaller shows, with plenty of room for a picnic.

Vehicles move along the fence here, and the view of anything on the second display line isn’t great. I would pick it for space rather than the best view.

  • SuitsPicnics and a calmer day
  • FacilitiesA longer walk back to the hangars
  • Watch outVehicles along the fence, and a poor view of the second display line

What about outside the aerodrome?I haven’t pinned anywhere outside. Hill Lane, which crosses the north end of the runway, is closed during the displays, and on show days the only way in is the Alder Drive gate off the B658, not through Old Warden village. The land around the aerodrome is a private estate, so please park in the show car parks and don’t stop on the lanes or block gateways. The pins are my best guess, and paddock positions can change, so please follow the stewards.

The airfield layout

The crowd stands in the paddocks along the western side of the grass runway, facing east across the airfield. You will find the hangars, Visitor Centre and Runway Café behind the northern paddocks, and the show entrance is to the south on Alder Drive.
