RIAT, the Royal International Air TattooParking & arrival guide

RIAT moves to MOD Boscombe Down, just south east of Amesbury in Wiltshire, with the A303 alongside. RIAT says the new site has better public transport links, with free rail shuttles from Salisbury, Andover and Swindon stations and Park and Ride planned. The routes, times and parking details aren’t out yet, so I would hold off on anything you can’t cancel until they are.

Good to know

How I would plan the day.

  • If you want a front row seat, head to the crowd line first and save your pitch, then look round the static park.
  • Visit the toilet before the flying starts. Use a display you aren’t fussed about as your break.
  • Your child’s ears are delicate, so try to protect them as much as possible with ear defenders.
  • Keep your ticket handy, as you’ll need it to board the free shuttles.

Good to know

Before the last display ends.

  • Agree a pick up time and place in advance, as phone signal can be poor in big crowds.
  • Look at the last shuttle back to your station before you set off.
  • Staying on for the static park can let the queues go down.
  • Hotels round Salisbury and Amesbury will go fast, so a room you can cancel for free might work well.
Do I need to book parking?
Not yet, as there’s no parking to book. The transport plan is still being worked out. If you pre paid for parking for the Fairford show, it will be refunded automatically to your card, £15 a car or £7.50 a motorbike, by the end of December 2026, and you’ll get priority booking if parking is offered at Boscombe Down.
When do the car parks open?
RIAT says the car parks will open at 06:00 each show day. I would check when they open nearer the show and try to arrive about an hour before you want to be on the crowd line.
Is there a shuttle from Fairford?
Nothing is confirmed from Fairford itself yet. RIAT says it will run a Swindon Shuttle to Boscombe Down, with the details coming with the transport plan. RIAT puts Swindon and Fairford at about an hour’s drive from Boscombe Down.
Should I book my train now?
I would hold off on anything you can’t cancel, like Advance train tickets, until the shuttle plans are out. Free rail shuttles are planned from Salisbury, Andover and Swindon stations.
Who do I ask about my own booking?
RIAT Customer Services is on 01285 713456, and RIAT’s Boscombe Down questions page answers the common ones.
