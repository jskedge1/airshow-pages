Your Booking And The Move

Bookings, including RIAT26 tickets rolled over to 2027, stay valid for Boscombe Down. This is what RIAT has said about refunds, parking, campsites and enclosures.

Thinking about a refund?

RIAT asks you to wait for the transport, Park & Ride, hotel and camping details first. If you still want one, use the refund form with your original booking reference.

Pre-paid parking

This will be refunded automatically to your card (£15 a car, £7.50 a motorbike) by the end of December 2026. If parking is offered at Boscombe Down, you’ll get priority booking.

Booked a campsite near Fairford?

If it was a non-refundable booking at an unofficial site, you can claim a free standard plot at the official campsite. How to claim is still to come.

Enclosure bookings

You’ll be contacted about the changes and your options to switch or cancel.

Shuttles from the Fairford area

RIAT says it will run a Swindon Shuttle to Boscombe Down. Nothing is confirmed from Fairford itself yet, and details will come with the transport plan. RIAT puts Swindon and Fairford at about an hour’s drive from Boscombe Down.

My advice for now

If you’re travelling a long way, hotels round Salisbury and Amesbury will go fast once people start planning, so a room you can cancel for free might work well for you. Hold off on anything non-refundable, like Advance train tickets, until the shuttle plans are out.
