Bus 132, Cambridge to IWM Duxford

This is the road the 132 takes from Cambridge city centre and the railway station to the bus stop by Hangar 1 at IWM Duxford, then on to Saffron Walden. It runs on Sundays and bank holidays only, about every two hours. Tap a stop to find it on the map.

Stops and times

    Cambridge city centreDrummer Street bus station, Regent Street and Hills Road (A1307), past Cambridge railway station.
    Addenbrooke’sLong Road (A1134) to the hospital bus station, then Robinson Way and Trumpington.
    Trumpington Park and RideHauxton Road (A1309) to the Park and Ride, then on to the M11.
    M11 to DuxfordSouth on the M11 to junction 10, then the A505 to the museum stop by Hangar 1.
    On to Saffron WaldenDuxford, Ickleton and Great Chesterford, then the B1383 and Audley End to Saffron Walden.
    I would advise checking the times before you travel. The 132 doesn’t run on Saturdays, and on Air Show weekends IWM usually runs its own shuttle from Cambridge, which I would suggest is the easier choice.
