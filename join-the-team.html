How It Works

From your first message to seeing your name on the site. There are no forms to fill in and no interview. Just a chat.

No rota. No pressure.

  1. Step 1

    Send Me A Message

    Tell me a bit about yourself, which shows you go to and what you'd like to help with. The message builder further down this page does most of the writing for you.

  2. Step 2

    We Have A Quick Chat

    I'll get back to you, usually by email. We talk about what you enjoy and how much time you've got. If that's two photos a year, that's fine by me.

  3. Step 3

    Pick Your Role And Your Shows

    Choose one role or a few. Most people stick to the shows they already go to, which is exactly what I want. Local knowledge is the good stuff.

  4. Step 4

    Send Your Work In

    Photos, video, a write up or a few tips. Once you are on the team, you send them through the Volunteer Hub, where you can also see when they go live. Big files can go in as a Google Drive or WeTransfer link.

    I would suggest telling me the show, the day and roughly where you were stood. It helps me put your work in the right place on the right page.
  5. Step 5

    It Goes Live With Your Name

    I check it over and add it to the guide, with your name on it. If anything needs changing, I'll ask you first rather than edit it quietly.

  6. Then

    Carry On, Or Take A Break

    Send more whenever you like. Busy season at work? Step back for a while. If you ever want something taken down, ask and I'll remove it.

A Few Things I Ask

Not many rules, I promise. These just keep the guide honest and keep you on the right side of each show.

✓

Please Do

  • Send work that's your ownYour photos, your words, your video.
  • Be honestIf the car park was a mess, say so. Readers trust that.
  • Tell me the show and the dateThings change year to year.
  • Follow each show's rulesPlease see the rules before you go.
!

Check With Me First

  • Close ups of peopleEspecially children. I'd rather check than guess.
  • Show logos and official materialI only use these with the organiser's permission.
  • Anything from behind the barriersMedia passes and private areas come with their own rules.
  • If you're under 18I'd need a parent or guardian to say it's OK.
×

Please Don't

  • Send other people's photosEven with credit, unless they've said yes.
  • Fly drones at a showIt isn't allowed, and it puts the display at risk.
  • Speak for the guide without askingOrganisers and press come to me first.
