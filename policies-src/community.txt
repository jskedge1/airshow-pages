COMMUNITY RULES

The Big Guide to UK Airshows
Forum, My Airshow Hub, Show Day and member uploads

Rules first published: 26 October 2026
Last updated: 26 October 2026

1. WHY THESE RULES EXIST

The forum and My Airshow Hub are here so people who love airshows can help each other: tips, photos, kit advice, car park updates and a friendly word for first timers.

These rules keep it that way. They apply to everything members post, upload, send or report on the site. By joining, you agree to them.

2. WHO CAN JOIN

You need to be 16 or over to have an account.

The show day buddy board, lift sharing, camping pitch sharing and any other arranged meet up are for adults aged 18 and over only.

3. BE A GOOD AIRSHOW NEIGHBOUR

Be kind, especially to beginners. Everyone started somewhere.
Disagree with the idea, not the person.
Keep posts about airshows, aviation, photography, camping, travel and the day out.
Give honest advice from your own experience.
Credit other people's ideas and photos.

4. WHAT IS NOT ALLOWED

Anything illegal, including threats, harassment, hate, terrorism content, fraud, scams and anything that sexualises or endangers children. I report illegal content to the police or the right authority.
Bullying, abuse or targeting anyone, including pilots, display teams, organisers, staff or other members.
Content that encourages self harm, suicide or eating disorders.
Sexual or violent images.
Sharing someone's private details, such as their home address, phone number or car registration, without their permission.
Photos or videos of children that are not your own family, unless the parent or carer has agreed. Wide crowd shots are fine.
Other people's photos, videos or writing posted as your own, or without permission. This includes copying whole pages from airshow programmes or official websites.
Fake tickets, ticket touting above face value, counterfeit goods or anything sold in breach of an event's rules.
Encouraging people to trespass, enter restricted areas, fly drones where they are not allowed or break airfield rules.
Spam, repeated adverts, fake accounts, pretending to be someone else, or fake reviews.
Selling or advertising without a trader listing, outside the kit for sale or swap board.

5. SHOW DAY UPLOADS AND CROWD REPORTS

Only send a photo of the flying display page when you are at the show, on the day.
Crowd reports about car parks, toilets and queues should be what you can see yourself, right now.
Never post anything that could compromise safety or security, such as restricted area access, security procedures or the live position of military aircraft on the ground.

6. MEETING UP WITH OTHER MEMBERS

The buddy board is a great way to share a lift or a pitch, but you are meeting someone you do not know. I would advise:

meet in a busy public place first, such as the main entrance or a food area
tell a friend or family member who you are meeting and where
keep your own transport home or a way to leave
never send money in advance for a lift or pitch
trust your gut and leave if something feels wrong

I do not check members' identities. Any arrangement you make is between you and the other person. If someone makes you uncomfortable, please report them.

7. HOW TO REPORT SOMETHING

Every post, comment, photo and profile has a report button. You do not need to give your name to the person you report, and they will not be told who reported them.

You can also email contactus@ukairshowguide.co.uk with a link or a screenshot. You do not need to be a member to report something.

If someone is in immediate danger, call 999 first.

8. WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

Every report goes straight into my admin queue and I look at each one myself. I aim to remove clearly illegal content as soon as I see it, and to review other reports within 48 hours. Show Day photos flagged by two members come off the board straight away while I check them.

Depending on what I find, I may:

leave it up if it does not break the rules
edit or remove the content
send you a warning
pause your account for a while
close your account and block you from rejoining
report illegal content to the police or the right authority

I keep a short record of reports and what I did, so I can deal with repeat problems fairly.

9. IF YOU DISAGREE WITH A DECISION

If your content was removed or your account was restricted and you think I got it wrong, email contactus@ukairshowguide.co.uk within 28 days and tell me why. I will look at it again and reply within 14 days.

You can also complain if you reported something and you are unhappy with what I did, or if you think I am not following these rules or my legal duties. I take freedom of speech and your privacy seriously when I make these decisions.

10. HOW I KEEP THE COMMUNITY SAFE

I have looked at the risks of illegal content on this site and written down what I do about them, as the Online Safety Act requires. That includes the age limits above, the report button, my admin queue, automatic checks on Show Day uploads, and my own moderation. I review this whenever I add a new feature and at least once a year.

11. YOUR CONTENT

You own what you post. The Website Terms explain the permission you give me to show it, and the Privacy Policy explains how I handle your information. You can delete your own posts and close your account at any time.

12. CHANGES

As the community grows I may update these rules. The date at the top shows when they last changed, and I will mention big changes in the forum.
