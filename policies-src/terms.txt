3. PLEASE CHECK BEFORE YOU TRAVEL

Airshows are live events and things change, sometimes on the day. Dates, times, aircraft, car parks, road closures, buses, trains, prices, campsites, accessibility arrangements and event rules can all move after I write about them.

I check my guides and update them when I hear of changes, and some details such as line ups and show dates update automatically from official sources. Even so, a page can be out of date.

Everything in my guides is advice from my own visits and research, not instructions you have to follow. Please choose what suits you and your family, and always follow the official show rules and any directions from staff, marshals and police on the day.

I would always advise checking the official airshow website, and your train, bus, campsite or hotel, before you set off. If the official information differs from mine, go with theirs.

Weather panels, sun position guides and flight briefings are for planning only. Check a current forecast on the day.

Live tools such as Show Day, crowd reports and the arrivals board rely on members and outside data. They can be late, wrong or missing, so treat them as a helping hand, not an official source.

5. YOUR SAFETY

Please stay on public or authorised land, follow signs, barriers and marshals, park legally and never block roads, gates or emergency access. No photo is worth going somewhere you should not.

Think about hearing protection for children, sun, rain, food, water and medication. You know your own family and needs best.
