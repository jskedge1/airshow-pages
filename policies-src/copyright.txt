4. AI GENERATED IMAGES

I want this website to stay professional, true and authentic, so I would rather show you real photos wherever I can.

There are a few things I have not been able to photograph yet. Some areas are behind security, closed to the public or only open on certain days, so I cannot just walk in and take a picture. Others are things I simply have not caught on camera yet.

For these, I have created a small number of AI generated images. They are there purely for a visual look, to help you picture what could happen on the day and how it may look, such as a shuttle bus stop or a layout of an area.

Every AI generated image on this site has a caption saying what it shows and that it is AI generated, so you always know which pictures are real photos and which are illustrations.
An AI image is not a photo of the real place. Details such as signs, colours, vehicles and layouts may be different on the day.
I will replace AI images with my own photos as soon as I am able to capture them.

Please do not copy or reuse the AI images either. If you think an AI image gives the wrong impression of a place or event, please email me and I will look at it straight away.

5. PHOTO DISCLAIMER

The photos on this site are here to help you plan your visit and to show what airshows can be like. Please keep in mind:

Aircraft, display teams and liveries change from year to year, so a photo is not a promise that an aircraft or team will appear at a show.
A photo of a viewing spot, car park or campsite shows how it looked on the day it was taken. Layouts, fencing and facilities can change.
Camera settings shown with a photo are what worked for that shot. Your results will depend on your own kit, the light and the aircraft.
Where people can be seen in a crowd shot, they are not the subject of the photo. If you are in a photo and would like it removed, email me and I will take it down.
