2. SHARING MY WORK

You are very welcome to share a link to any page or post on social media.

Please do not copy guides, photos, maps or tools onto another website, into a group post, a printed guide or anything you sell without asking me first. If you would like to use something, email me. I am usually happy to help airshow clubs, schools and charities.

3. FACTS AND OFFICIAL INFORMATION

Show dates, aircraft line ups and similar facts come from official sources and are written up in my own words, with a link to the official website. I do not copy official text, programmes or photos without permission.

4. THINK SOMETHING OF YOURS IS HERE?

If you believe a photo, video or piece of writing on this site is yours and is here without your permission, or the credit is wrong, please email contactus@ukairshowguide.co.uk with:

a link to the page
which item is yours
how I can confirm it is yours, such as the original file or a link to where you first published it
what you would like me to do: credit it, change it or remove it

I will take it down while I check, usually within 2 working days, and reply to let you know the outcome. This also applies to anything posted by members in the forum.

5. VOLUNTEERS AND CONTRIBUTORS

The site is helped by volunteers: airshow experts, photographers, videographers, writers and social media helpers. Contribute when you can. Cover what you know.

Taking part is voluntary and unpaid, with no minimum commitment, and it does not make you an employee.

You keep the copyright in everything you send me.
You give me permission to publish it on the site and its social media pages, and to edit it for length, layout or clarity without changing its meaning.
You are credited as you choose, or not at all if you prefer.
You can ask me to remove your work at any time, and I will take it down from the site within 14 days. Posts already shared on social media may stay up, but I will remove them where I can.
Please only send work that is yours, and photos of people who are happy to appear in them.

If we agree anything different in writing, such as for a particular project, that agreement takes priority.

6. AIRSHOW ORGANISERS

If you run a show, I am always happy to hear from you. I can correct details, add official links, include your logos and photos with your permission, and promote your event free of charge. Nothing on this site is official unless you and I have agreed it and the page says so.
