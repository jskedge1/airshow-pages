1. THE SHORT VERSION

Cookies are small files a website saves on your phone, tablet or computer. Some are needed for the site to work. Some help me see which guides are useful. A few come from services I show on the page, such as Facebook and YouTube.

I do not use advertising cookies and I do not follow you around the internet.

This page explains each type and how you can turn them off. It goes with my Privacy Policy.

2. WHAT ELSE COUNTS AS A COOKIE

The same rules cover similar tools, such as your browser's local storage, which saves small notes on your own device. On this page I call them all cookies to keep things simple.

3. COOKIES THE SITE NEEDS

These keep the site running and safe. They let you log in and stay logged in, protect forms from spam, balance traffic and remember your cookie choices.

The site cannot work properly without them, so the law does not ask me to get your permission first. If you block them in your browser, logging in and some tools will stop working.

4. REMEMBERING YOUR CHOICES

The site saves a few small notes on your own device so it remembers how you like things. For example:

whether you chose the Clean or HUD look for the forum
whether the hub sounds are on or off
which area you picked on the airshow finder
whether you have already seen the hub welcome screen or the add to home screen tip

These stay on your device. I cannot see them and they are not used to track you. The law allows these without permission because they only change how the site looks or works for you. You can clear them at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

5. SITE STATISTICS

The website platform counts visits so I can see which pages people read, how they found the site, what device they use and where pages are slow or broken.

I only use this to improve the site. The figures are added up into totals, I do not use them for advertising and I do not share them for anyone else's use.

Since February 2026, UK law allows statistics cookies like these without asking first, as long as I tell you about them and give you a free and simple way to say no. You can say no at any time using the cookie banner or cookie settings on the site, or by blocking cookies in your browser. Saying no will not stop you using any part of the site.

6. COOKIES FROM OTHER SERVICES

Some pages show content from other companies. They set their own cookies, which they control:

YouTube videos are shown in privacy enhanced mode, so YouTube should not set cookies until you press play.
Facebook feeds on show pages and the news page come from Meta, which may set cookies when the feed loads, including on people who do not use Facebook.
Google Maps links and calendar buttons take you to Google, where Google's own cookies apply.
Payments, if you sign up to a paid plan, are handled by the website platform's payment providers.

Where the law says these need your permission, I will ask before they load. To find out what each company does, please read its own privacy and cookie policy.

7. ADVERTISING

There are no advertising or tracking cookies on the site at the moment.

Some guides link to shops or booking sites. Where I might earn a small commission from a link, it is labelled. Clicking it takes you to that shop, and their cookies apply from then on.

If I ever add advertising cookies, they will stay off until you choose to switch them on.

8. HOW TO CONTROL COOKIES

Use the cookie banner when it appears, or the cookie settings option on the site, to accept or refuse optional cookies. You can change your mind whenever you like.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Every browser lets you do this, and most have a private browsing mode too.

9. MY SISTER SITE

The Big Guide to RIAT (www.thebigguidetoriat.co.uk) has its own cookies and settings. Choices you make here do not carry across.

10. CHANGES

I will update this page when I add or remove a service that uses cookies. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

11. CONTACT
