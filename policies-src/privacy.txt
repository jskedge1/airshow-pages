PRIVACY POLICY

The Big Guide to UK Airshows
Also known as UK Airshow Guide

Website: www.ukairshowguide.co.uk
Policy first published: 1 July 2026
Last updated: 31 October 2026

1. THE SHORT VERSION

I built this site to help people plan a good day out at a UK airshow, and I would like you to feel comfortable using it.

You can read every guide without giving me anything about yourself.

If you join as a member, post in the forum, use the planning tools, send me a message or list a business, I keep what you give me so those parts of the site work for you.

I do not sell your information. I do not use it for advertising. You can ask to see it, change it or delete it at any time by emailing contactus@ukairshowguide.co.uk.

The rest of this page explains it all in more detail.

2. WHO I AM

UK Airshow Guide is run by me, Jack Skedge, from Essex in England. I am the person responsible for your information, which the law calls the data controller.

Some volunteers help me with guides, photos and the forum. Where a volunteer helps me moderate or answer messages, they only see what they need for that job and they follow this policy.

The site has a sister site, The Big Guide to RIAT (www.thebigguidetoriat.co.uk). It has its own policies. Joining one site does not sign you up to the other.

3. WHAT I COLLECT AND WHY

Browsing the site
When you visit, the website platform records basic technical details such as your IP address, browser, device type, the pages you look at and roughly where in the world you are. I use this to keep the site secure, fix problems and see which guides people find useful.

Messages and the contact form
If you contact me, I keep your name, email address and what you write so I can reply. Sending a message does not add you to any mailing list.

Member accounts
To join you give an email address and a password, and you can add a name, a profile photo and a short bio. Passwords are handled by the website platform. I never see them.

The forum and My Airshow Hub
If you take part, I keep what you post: threads, comments, photos, likes, follows, groups you join, badges you earn and the notifications the site sends you. Your profile name, photo and posts can be seen by other members, and some posts can be seen by visitors who are not logged in. Please think before you share anything personal in a public post.

Planning tools
The trip planner, show checklist, must see aircraft list, My Hangar, saved tools, budget tracker, packing lists, past shows and your settings are private to you. I only look at them if you ask me to help with a problem.

Show Day uploads and crowd reports
If you send a photo of a flying display page, the site checks it is for the right show and the right day. To do that it asks your phone for its location, but only when you press send. Your location is used for that one check. I keep the result, such as about 1 km from the crowd line, not your exact position.
Crowd reports about car parks, toilets and queues are linked to your account so the site can stop spam.

Using your location on the maps and bus pages
The airshow map and the bus route pages have buttons that ask your phone for its location. Nothing is asked until you press one. On the map, your location is used to find shows near you and is rounded to the nearest town name. On a bus route page, the On the bus screen can follow your location to show how far along the route you are. That happens in your browser, I do not receive it and I do not store it. You can stop it with the Stop using my location button or by closing the page.

Photos you upload
When you upload a photo, the site reads the camera settings so it can show them, then saves a fresh, resized copy. That copy drops hidden details such as where the photo was taken.

Traders and partners
If you list a business, I keep your business name, contact details, the shows you attend, your description, logo and photos. Your listing is public once I have checked it. If paid plans are switched on, payments are taken by the website platform and its payment providers. I never see your full card details.

Volunteers, contributors and organisers
If you offer to help or send me content, I keep your contact details, what you have sent, how you would like to be credited and any permissions you have given me.

Social media
If you message or comment on my Facebook, Instagram, YouTube or TikTok pages, those platforms also handle your information under their own policies.

4. MY LEGAL REASONS

The law asks me to say which reason I rely on each time.

Contract: running your member account, the forum, the planning tools and any trader plan you sign up to.

Legitimate interests: keeping the site safe, replying to messages, stopping spam and abuse, checking Show Day uploads and improving the guides. I only rely on this where it does not override your rights.

Consent: optional cookies, any newsletter, emails about things you have asked for, and publishing work you send me. You can take consent back at any time.

Legal obligation: keeping records I must keep, for example for tax, or dealing with illegal content under the Online Safety Act.

5. WHO ELSE HANDLES YOUR INFORMATION

I use a small number of trusted services to run the site:

Wix, which hosts the website, member accounts, the forum database, forms, chat and payments.
GitHub, which hosts some of the page designs. Like any web host, it sees your IP address when a page loads.
Google Fonts, which supplies the Barlow typeface. Your browser asks Google for the font, so Google sees your IP address.
Open Meteo, which supplies weather forecasts. The site asks it for the weather at the airshow, not at your location.
Live train and bus information providers, when you open a route page. The pages ask National Rail departure board services and bustimes.org for live times, and they see your IP address when they do. Route maps use OpenStreetMap and a code library from unpkg, which also see your IP address when the map loads.
YouTube and Facebook, when you choose to watch an embedded video or view an embedded Facebook feed.
My email provider, for replying to you.

I do not share your information with airshow organisers, traders or anyone else for their own marketing.

I would only pass information to the police or another authority where the law requires it, or where someone is at risk of serious harm.

6. INFORMATION OUTSIDE THE UK

Some of these services store information in other countries, including the United States and the European Union. Where that happens, they use the protections UK law requires, such as approved contract terms or the UK and US data bridge.

7. HOW LONG I KEEP THINGS

Member accounts: until you close your account. Your information is then removed within 30 days, apart from anything I must keep by law.
Forum posts and photos: until you delete them or close your account. If you would like your posts removed too, just ask.
Messages to me: up to 2 years, then deleted, unless they are part of an ongoing arrangement.
Show Day uploads: shown on the board on the day only. Held photos are kept for up to 90 days so I can check them, then deleted.
Crowd reports: shown for 45 minutes and deleted after 30 days.
Trader records and payments: up to 6 years after you stop, because that is what tax rules expect.
Reports of illegal content: as long as needed to deal with them properly.

8. CHILDREN

The guides are written for families and anyone can read them.

Member accounts, the forum and uploads are for people aged 16 and over. If you are 16 or 17, please talk to a parent or carer before you join.

Meeting up with other members through the forum, such as sharing lifts or camping pitches, is for adults aged 18 and over only.

If I find out someone under 16 has made an account, I will close it and delete their information. If you think this has happened, please tell me.

9. YOUR RIGHTS

You have the right to:

ask for a copy of the information I hold about you
ask me to correct anything that is wrong
ask me to delete your information
ask me to limit how I use it
object to how I use it, including any marketing
ask for your information in a format you can take elsewhere
take back any consent you have given

Email contactus@ukairshowguide.co.uk and I will reply within one month. It is free. I may ask you to confirm who you are first, so I do not hand your information to the wrong person.

You can also change or delete most of your profile, posts and planning tools yourself from your account.

10. KEEPING IT SAFE

Member areas are protected by logins, the planning tools are private by default, and only I have admin access. No website can promise to be completely secure, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

If something ever went wrong that put your information at risk, I would tell you and, where the law requires it, the Information Commissioner's Office.

11. COMPLAINTS

If you are unhappy with how I have handled your information, please tell me first and I will do my best to put it right.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK regulator for data protection, at ico.org.uk or by calling 0303 123 1113.

12. CHANGES

I will update this page as the site grows. If I make a big change that affects members, I will let you know on the site before it starts.
