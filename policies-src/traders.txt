1. WHO THESE TERMS ARE FOR

These terms apply if you list a business in the airshow traders directory, sign up as a stay, transport or other partner, advertise on the site, or buy any paid plan or supporter membership. They sit alongside the Website Terms, the Community Rules and the Privacy Policy.

2. YOUR LISTING

You must be the owner of the business, or allowed to act for it.
Everything in your listing must be true and kept up to date, including the shows you attend, prices you mention, opening times and contact details.
Your logo, photos and words must be yours or used with permission.
You must hold any licences, insurance, food hygiene rating or trader pitch you need for the shows you list. Being on this site is not permission to trade at any airshow. That is between you and each organiser.
You are responsible for your own products, prices, bookings, refunds and customer service.

3. CHECKING LISTINGS

I check every new listing and every change before it goes live. I can ask you to change something, pause a listing or remove it if it is untrue, out of date, breaks the law or the Community Rules, or could mislead visitors. If I remove a paid listing for a serious breach, I may not refund the time left.

4. FREE LISTINGS

Free listings stay up while they are kept accurate. I may change what a free listing includes, and I will tell you before I do.

5. PAID PLANS

Paid plans are not on sale yet. When they are, the price, what is included, how long the plan lasts and how it renews will be shown clearly before you pay.

Payments are taken securely by the website platform and its payment providers.
Plans that renew will say so before you pay, and I will remind you before a yearly plan renews.
You can cancel renewal at any time from your account or by emailing me. Your plan then runs to the end of the period you have paid for.
Founding trader places, where offered, are limited in number and the offer price lasts for as long as the offer says.
Prices include VAT if VAT applies.

6. YOUR RIGHT TO CANCEL

If you buy as a private person, for example a supporter membership, you have 14 days from the day you buy to change your mind and get a full refund. If you ask me to start the plan straight away and then cancel within the 14 days, I may keep a fair amount for the time already used.

If you buy for a business, the 14 day cancellation right does not apply by law, but you can still cancel renewal at any time.

To cancel, email contactus@ukairshowguide.co.uk with your name and the plan.

7. IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

If the site is down for a long time or I cannot provide what your plan includes, I will put it right or give you a fair refund for the time affected.

What I cannot promise is a number of visitors, enquiries or sales. Airshows can be cancelled or changed by organisers, and that is outside my control. If a show you are listed for is cancelled, your listing simply shows your other dates.

8. ENDING A PLAN

If I ever stop offering a paid plan, I will give you at least 30 days' notice and refund any time you have paid for but will not get.

9. YOUR INFORMATION

The Privacy Policy explains how I handle your details, including payment records, which I keep for 6 years for tax.

10. CHANGES

I may update these terms. If a change affects a plan you have already paid for, it will only apply from your next renewal, and I will tell you before then.
