# Two Days, Same Airshow: shot list (as built)

140 beats at 80 bpm = 105 s, 16x9 only. Split screen, each half a 960 px stage.
Left = "Without a plan" (greyer, busier). Right = "With a plan" (normal warm palette).
Fixed furniture from beat 8: a label on each half, a "Charlie's energy" battery on each half,
a clock in the divider. The half that is not playing is lightly veiled so the eye knows where to look.
Seconds = beats x 0.75.

## Changes from the brief, and why
- Moment 1 plays right half first and the clock rolls 09:00 to 11:00 inside it. The brief had the left family arriving at 11 under a 09:00 clock; running that one moment in clock order keeps the clock honest. Moment 2 is therefore 11:30, not 10:00.
- Each moment has a small numbered title card ("1 Arriving") before its tip card, so the bottom of the frame is never empty and the number carries through.
- New 20-beat section for people standing nearby (client change), so the film is 105 s.
- Square corners on every flat item (client house rule). Aircraft are the kit's cut-outs only.

## Sound
Soft tok / blip / tick / pop / bell only, gain 0.25 or less. A falling pop when the left battery drains, a
small bell when the plan works on the right, a tick when the clock steps, one very quiet low whoosh under
the left-hand jet (the right-hand jet is silent on purpose). No impact, crash, riser or thud.
