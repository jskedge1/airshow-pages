# The Captain's Checklist: shot list (as built)

140 beats at 80 bpm = 105 s, 16x9. One beat = 0.75 s. Charlie plans the airshow day the way a
display pilot plans a flight and is the captain of the day. A six-box progress strip sits top left
from beat 12.6 to the end card, ticking at the end of each item (24.6, 36.6, 48.6, 60.6, 72.6, 84.6).
All corners are square (house rule). Aircraft are only the kit's cut-outs (Typhoon, Hawk, Mustang).
