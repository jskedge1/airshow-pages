For beginners
Why are my propellers frozen?

Shot the Spitfire at 1/2000 and the prop looks stopped dead. What should I try?

I would suggest dropping to 1/250 for warbirds, then 1/160 once you are steady. Take bursts and expect a few soft ones.

Answered by Jack
Too dark against a bright sky

Every Typhoon comes out as a black shape.

Try plus 1 exposure compensation, or spot meter on the jet. This will work well when the sky is flat grey.
