The founder

Hello, I'm the one you'll hear from on most pages of this guide.

JS
Creator and Managing Editor

Jack Skedge

I've been going to the Royal International Air Tattoo since 2006. Over the years I've made plenty of planning mistakes of my own, and I learnt most of what's in this guide the hard way.

That's really why this guide exists. I wanted one place where you could plan any UK airshow properly, with honest advice on getting there, where to sit, what to pack and whether a show suits you and your family. I write the guides, build the pages and keep the show information checked through the year.
