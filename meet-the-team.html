The founder

Hello, I'm the one you'll hear from on most pages of this guide.

JS
Creator and Managing Editor

Jack Skedge

I created The Big Guide to UK Airshows in 2026, after building The Big Guide to RIAT. I've been going to airshows for many years, and I've made plenty of mistakes along the way, from poor planning to turning up unprepared.

Those trips showed me just how much difference good information makes. So this guide brings it all together in one place: travel, parking, photography, places to stay, what to bring and much more. I write the guides, build the pages and keep the show information checked through the year.

I also wanted it to be something the airshow community can be part of. There's a huge amount of knowledge out there, and the more of us there are, the better the guide gets.

Ways you can help

Airshow expert
