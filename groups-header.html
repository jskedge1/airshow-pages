CHOOSE A GROUP

Each group is a topic. Open one, press Join, and you can start threads and reply straight away. If you are new to airshows, I would suggest starting in First timers and families, then saying hello in the welcome group.

HOUSE RULES

A friendly place for everyone who loves airshows, from first timers to seasoned spotters.

  1. Be kind and helpful. Everyone was a first timer once.
  2. Keep posts in the right group so they are easy to find.
  3. Only share photos and videos you took yourself, or have permission to share.
  4. Do not post personal details, yours or anyone else's.
  5. No selling outside the Kit, gear and buy and sell group, and no advertising without asking first.
  6. Show times, line ups and parking can change. Always check the official show website before you travel.
  7. Report anything that breaks these rules and the guide team will take a look.

SELLING KIT

Start your title with Selling or Wanted, add a clear photo, the price and your area, and agree the sale by private message.

I would advise meeting somewhere public, checking the item before you pay, and using a payment method with buyer protection.

Posts that break the rules may be removed, and repeat offenders may be removed from the community.
