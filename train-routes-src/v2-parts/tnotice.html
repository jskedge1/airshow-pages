These are this winter’s train times

Train companies change their timetables twice a year, and the summer one is not the same as the winter one. Some trains run at different times, and a few get added or taken away. A change that works well now may not work in summer.

Every time on this page comes from a winter weekend, so please treat it as a guide to how the trains run. When I last checked, the summer 2027 times had not been published. They usually come out in the spring, and I will update this page when they do. I have kept to the trains and changes that work well, so a journey planner may show a few more options.
