Making it real

What to start with and what a supplier will ask for.

Suggested first range

  • Flightline Crew round patch. Low cost per unit, fits an envelope, and is the item airshow people collect.
  • Sticker sheet and button badges. Pennies each, so they work as giveaways and order extras.
  • Navy logo tee and patch cap. Print on demand first, so there is no stock to hold.
  • Classic mug. The easy gift item, also print on demand.
  • Display Season shield. Bring it out each spring as that year's edition.

Before ordering

  • The logo needs a vector version. The current file is a 1497 pixel PNG, which prints cleanly up to about 12 cm wide. Embroidery, enamel and large back prints need it redrawn as SVG or AI.
  • Patches drop the strapline. The small “by The Big Guide to UK Airshows” line is too fine to stitch below about 12 cm, so the patch designs here leave it out or use woven instead.
  • Two logo versions. Navy text for white and pale items, white text for navy and dark items.
  • Show and team names. These designs use only your own name and artwork. Naming a specific show or display team on a product would need their permission.
