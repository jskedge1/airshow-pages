How The Forum Works

Free to join, and run by airshow fans

I'd say half the fun of an airshow is the chat along the barrier. Who's flying, which car park empties first, where the light is best by four o'clock. The forum puts that chat in one place, so you can ask people who have been before.

This page shows you round. How you join, where to post, and how I keep it friendly.

Meet, Spot And Swap

The bits that make it feel like a club rather than a message board.

Buddy boardGoing on your own? Post the show and the day, and find someone to meet, share a lift or pitch next to.
Members pageFans who choose to be listed, with who is going to the same shows as you at the top. Tap anyone to see their profile and follow them.
Private messagesTap a name on any post, then Message. The chat sits at the bottom of your screen while you read. You choose who can message you, and you can block anyone.
Rarity logSpotted something rare? Log it with the show and the year so everyone can see what turned up.
Kit swapMembers selling or swapping cameras, lenses and camping kit. You sort payment and collection between you.
Staying safe. I would advise meeting in a busy place first, like the main entrance, and never paying by bank transfer to someone you haven't met. If a chat feels off, block them and report it from the three dots at the top of the chat.

Good To Know

The questions people ask me most about the forum. If yours isn't here, drop me a message.

Ask me something →
Can I read the forum without joining?

Yes. Anyone can read the threads. You only need an account to post, reply, like or share a photo.

Is it the same account as My Airshow Hub?

It is. One free account opens both, so you only log in once.

Can I delete something I posted?

Yes. Open your thread or reply and tap Delete. It comes off straight away.

What happens when I report a post?

It goes into my admin queue. I read it, then either keep it or remove it. The person who posted it isn't told who reported it.

Can other members message me?

Yes, any member can send you a private message unless you change it. In the Settings tab of My Profile you can pick any member, only people you follow, or nobody. Only you and the other person can read a chat, and you can block someone at any time.

Do I have to be on the Members page?

No. You are only listed if you switch it on, either on the Members page itself or in My Profile settings. People can still tap your name on your posts to see your profile.

Can I suggest a new group?

Please do. Tap Suggest a group at the bottom of the Groups section and tell me what it's for. I check each one before it goes live.

Is the forum free?

Yes, and there are no adverts in it. The guide earns a little through links and partners elsewhere on the site, so the forum can stay free.
