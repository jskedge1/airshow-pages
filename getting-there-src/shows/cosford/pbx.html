Good to know

How I would plan the day.

  • If you want a front row seat, head to the crowd line as soon as you’re in and save your pitch, then explore the static park.
  • Visit the toilet before the flying starts. Use a display you aren’t fussed about as your break.
  • The Typhoon is very loud over an airfield. Your child’s ears are delicate, so try to protect them as much as possible with ear defenders.
  • There’s almost no shade on the crowd line, so bring sun cream and a hat, and some cash in case the card machines lose signal.

Good to know

Before the last display ends.

  • Agree a pick up time and place in advance, as phone signal is poor on the showground with 50,000 people using it.
  • Book a return taxi in advance, and ask for a wheelchair accessible car when you book if you need one.
  • In 2026 the one way system reversed around 13:00, so you could leave early through Gate 2.
  • If you can, I would travel against the flow, as trains towards Wolverhampton and Birmingham are the busiest.
