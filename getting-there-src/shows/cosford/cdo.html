Parking on the airfield is included in your ticket, so there's nothing to pay on the day. The only paid parking I know of is outside the airfield at the Albrighton Trust, which is for anyone who missed out on tickets.

Vehicles on the airfield must be under 7 metres long and 7.5 tonnes, and Blue Badge spaces are first come first served. The Albrighton Trust opened from 09:00, was booked through Ticket Tailor and isn’t an official viewing area, so you won’t see the static park or hear the commentary. These were the 2026 prices, so I would check them again before you go.
