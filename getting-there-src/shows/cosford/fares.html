Under 16s | Free

Children go free with a paying adult, but you need to say how many are coming when you book. That makes it one of the best value big shows for a family.

Parking | Included

Parking on the airfield is included in your ticket, so there’s nothing extra to pay if you drive in.

Family & Friends Railcard | 1/3 off

Takes a third off adult rail fares and 60% off kids. I would say it pays for itself if you travel as a family, and under 5s travel free with a paying adult.

Sunday busesNoneNo 891 on show day
  • Banga Buses route 891 links Wolverhampton, Albrighton, Cosford, Shifnal and Telford from Monday to Saturday, but there are no Sunday buses, so it is no help on show day.
  • The train is far easier. If you are travelling on another day, I would check Traveline for the current timetable.
National ExpressNot listedBook in advance
  • No coach stops at Cosford, so I would take a National Express coach into Birmingham Coach Station and finish with the train. This can work out a lot cheaper than the train the whole way.
  • Lots of long distance coaches get in late, so I would think about travelling on the Saturday and staying over.
