Good to know

How I would plan the day.

  • If you want a front row spot, save your pitch on the seafront first, then head to the village in the morning while the flying is quiet.
  • Visit the toilet before the flying starts. Use a display you aren’t fussed about as your break.
  • The Typhoon is very loud. Your child’s ears are delicate, so try to protect them as much as possible with ear defenders.
  • The beach is shingle and hard going, so I would use the promenade or the Western Lawns with wheelchairs and pushchairs.

Good to know

Before the last display ends.

  • Agree a pick up time and place in advance, as phone signal can be poor in big crowds.
  • Five Acres Field is locked overnight, so I would be back at the car in good time.
  • Up at Beachy Head, check the time of the last 13X back down before you settle in, as the walk to the station is a long one.
  • Buses back to Brighton fill up, so I would allow extra time or stay in town for something to eat first, as this has worked for me.
