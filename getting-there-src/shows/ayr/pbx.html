Good to know

How I would plan the day.

  • If you want a front row seat, head to the Esplanade railings first and save your pitch.
  • Visit the toilet before the first display, as the queues build quickly once the crowds arrive.
  • Ear defenders aren’t handed out at Ayr, so bring your own. Your child’s ears are delicate.
  • There’s no cash machine on site, so I would bring a little cash in case the card machines lose signal.

Good to know

Before the last display ends.

  • Agree a pick up point in town, for example near Ayr station or the bus station on Sandgate, as the seafront streets are closed.
  • Book your return taxi in advance, as phone signal is poor with so many people on the seafront.
  • On the Friday the flying finishes in the dusk, so I would bring a small torch.
  • If you came by coach to Glasgow, leave a good gap before your coach home, as the roads out of Ayr are slow after the show.
