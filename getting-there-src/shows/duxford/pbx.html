Good to know

How I would plan the day.

  • If you want a front row spot, head to the crowd line first and save your pitch, then do the flightline walk before it closes at about 12 noon.
  • Eat a little early and visit the toilet before the flying starts, as the queues build quickly.
  • Engines are loud, especially on the flightline. I would advise proper ear defenders for little ones, not just cotton wool.
  • There’s no cashpoint on site and some traders only take cash, so I would bring a little with you.

Good to know

Before the last display ends.

  • Leave before the flying display ends, or stay on, look round the museum hangars and grab some food while the rush leaves. This has worked for me in the past.
  • The car parks are big grass fields, so I would drop a pin on your phone’s map when you park.
  • Agree a pick up point and time with your taxi driver, and allow for traffic leaving the site.
  • Leaving westbound, follow the event signs back onto the A505 rather than the B roads through Fowlmere and Thriplow.
