At the Summer and Battle of Britain Air Shows every car needs a parking permit, booked when you buy your tickets, and you can’t turn up and park without one. I haven’t been able to find a price for the permit, so I would check it on IWM’s site when you book. At the smaller events parking is free on site.

Spaces are limited at the big Air Shows, so I would book your permit as early as you can, and an accessible permit if you need one. The parking set up and the 2026 shuttle times are the latest I have, so I would check them again once IWM publishes the 2027 details.
