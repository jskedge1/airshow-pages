Good to know

How I would plan the day.

  • If you want a front row spot, head to the seafront first and save your pitch, then walk Marine Drive and the Military Village.
  • Visit the toilet before midday, as the queues build quickly once the first aircraft appear.
  • Your child’s ears are delicate, so try to protect them as much as possible with ear defenders. The Typhoon is very loud over the beach.
  • The sea comes in quickly, so check the tide times before you walk out on the sand.

Good to know

Before the last display ends.

  • Taxis can’t drive along the closed part of Marine Drive, so agree a pick up point and time in advance, as phone signal can be poor in big crowds.
  • In 2025 the Esplanade ran one way northbound until 15:00, then southbound for the journey out.
  • I would fill up with fuel on the way in rather than on the way home, when the queues out of town are long.
  • Think about your journey home before you set off, as Sunday trains and buses are less frequent.
