- 2027 dates not announced; 2026 show Saturday 29 and Sunday 30 August: page, Official.
- Car parks 09:00, gates 10:00, flying about 12:00 to 17:00: 2026, page and Official.

## Parking
- Four official car parks (Marine Drive/Promenade, Esplanade, Princes Park, Victoria Park), £10 per car per day, paid on arrival, open 09:00, about five minutes' walk; Blue Badge first come first served at £10; hard standing Esplanade and Marine Drive, grass Princes Park and Victoria Park; no park and ride; no pre booking: 2026, topic car, existing #cpk block, Questions; Official confirms £10, the four car parks and 9am. Neither the page nor Official says who runs them, so I called them "official car parks" rather than council car parks.
- Coach groups to the Esplanade: 2026, topic coach.
