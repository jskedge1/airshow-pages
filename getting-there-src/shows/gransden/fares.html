Under 5s | Free

Under 5s get into the show free, and they travel free on the train with a paying adult. Your child’s ears are delicate, so pack ear defenders.

Family ticket | £100

Covers 2 adults and 2 children for 2027, which works out cheaper than four separate tickets. I would book early, before the early bird prices end in August.

Family & Friends Railcard | 1/3 off

Takes a third off adult rail fares and 60% off kids, if you’re coming by train to St Neots or Sandy. I would say it pays for itself if you travel as a family.
