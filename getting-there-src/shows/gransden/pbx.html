Good to know

How I would plan the day.

  • If you want a front row seat, head to the crowd line first and save your pitch, then walk the car lines and the stalls.
  • Visit the toilet before the flying starts at around 12:30. Use a display you aren’t fussed about as your break.
  • The organisers recommend ear defenders for younger children. Your child’s ears are delicate, so try to protect them as much as possible.
  • Keep your wristband on, as you need it to get back in if you leave and come back.

Good to know

Before the last display ends.

  • Agree a pick up time with your taxi before the flying ends, and allow for traffic leaving the airfield.
  • Rural taxis are limited on show days, so I’d book both journeys before the day.
  • When you leave by car, follow the signs back to the B1046 and the A1198 rather than your sat nav.
  • If you’re staying for the After Show Party, sort out a driver or a taxi before you go. It finished at 23:00 in 2026 and the lanes are dark and narrow.
