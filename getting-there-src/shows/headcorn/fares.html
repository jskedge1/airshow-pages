Parking | Free

General parking is free on grass fields beside the site, first come first served, with no booking.

Aged 5 and under | Free

Children aged 5 and under go free with a standard adult booking. Your child’s ears are delicate, so pack ear defenders.

Family & Friends Railcard | 1/3 off

Takes a third off adult rail fares and 60% off kids on the train to Headcorn. I would say it pays for itself if you travel as a family.
