Good to know

How I would plan the day.

  • If you want a front row spot, head to the crowd line first and save your pitch, then look round the static aircraft and the traders.
  • Use the late morning for food and the toilet, and be back on your pitch before the flying starts at about 12:00.
  • Your child’s ears are delicate, so try to protect them as much as possible with ear defenders, and baby ear muffs for babies.
  • The car parks all look the same at 18:00, so I always drop a pin on my phone’s map when I park.

Good to know

Before the last display ends.

  • I would either leave before the flying ends, or stay on, look round the traders and grab some food while the rush leaves.
  • Book a taxi back to the station before the day, as the rank can empty quickly when the show ends.
  • Agree a pick up point with your driver, such as the main entrance, and allow for traffic leaving the car parks.
  • Overnight parking isn’t allowed in the day car parks, so book the airfield campsite if you want to stay.
