Good to know

How I would plan the day.

  • If you want a front row seat, head to the promenade or the beach first and save your pitch. The crowd centre by the Cenotaph fills first.
  • Visit the toilet before the flying starts. In 2026 there was a half hour break on the Saturday and about an hour on the Sunday, which is your chance.
  • Ear defenders aren’t handed out and the Typhoon is very loud, so bring your own for little ones.
  • Swansea Bay has a tidal range of up to 10.4 metres, so I would check the tide times before you set up on the sand.

Good to know

Before the last display ends.

  • Agree a pick up time and place in advance, as phone signal can be poor in big crowds.
  • Book a taxi home well ahead, especially after the evening show.
  • Expect big queues after the fireworks, so I would plan your journey home before you set off.
  • Bring a warm layer and a small torch if you stay for the evening show and the walk back.
