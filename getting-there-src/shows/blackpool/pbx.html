Good to know

How I would plan the day.

  • If you want a front row seat, head to the sea wall railings first and save your pitch.
  • Visit the toilet before the flying starts, as the Undercroft toilets by North Pier get very busy once the crowds build.
  • The Typhoon is very loud, so I would advise ear defenders for babies, toddlers and anyone sensitive to noise.
  • The beach between North Pier and Central Pier is cleared a bit before the flying, so head south of Central Pier if you want to be on the sand.

Good to know

Before the last display ends.

  • Agree a pick up time and place in advance, as phone signal is poor with so many people on the prom.
  • Pick up points need to be in town, for example near Blackpool North station or on Talbot Road, as the prom was closed to traffic until 19:00 in 2026.
  • Leaving before the final act can work if you have a connection to make.
  • The village stayed open until 18:00 in 2026, and a wander round it beats sitting in a queue for the M55.
