Good to know

How I would plan the day.

  • Arrive around 08:00 to be near the front of the holding queue, then if you want a front row spot, head to the crowd line first and save your pitch.
  • Food queues are longest just before the flying starts at about 14:00, so eat early and visit the toilet before then.
  • There are no published times for each display, so I wouldn’t wander too far once the flying starts.
  • Your child’s ears are delicate, so try to protect them as much as possible with ear defenders.

Good to know

Before the last display ends.

  • I would wait 30 to 45 minutes with a flask and the last of the picnic. In my experience it gets you home just as quickly and with far less stress.
  • The car parks look very different after dark at the evening shows, so drop a pin on your phone’s map when you park.
  • If you’re on the shuttle and want to see everything, think about booking a later return time.
  • Agree a pick up point and time with your taxi driver, and allow for traffic leaving the site.
