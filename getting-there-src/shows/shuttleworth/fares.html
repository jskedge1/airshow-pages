Under 18s | Free

Under 18s go free at every show apart from the Flying Proms, where children aged 4 and over pay £22.

Parking | Free

Parking is free at every 2027 show and you don’t need to book it. At most shows your ticket also covers the hangars, the Swiss Garden and the fun fair.

Carers | Free ticket

Carers of disabled visitors get a free ticket, booked by emailing enquiries@shuttleworth.org. I would bring proof, as they do random checks on the day.
