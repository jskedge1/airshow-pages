Good to know

How I would plan the day.

  • If you want a front row seat, head to the crowd line first and put your chairs or a blanket down. Gazebos aren’t allowed, but a small pop up tent is.
  • Visit the toilet before the air display begins. Use a display you aren’t waiting to see as your break.
  • Jets and balloon burners can be loud, so I would advise ear defenders for babies and toddlers.
  • A 05:30 start in June can still be chilly, so I would pack layers and a flask for the dawn balloons.

Good to know

Before the last display ends.

  • Book your return taxi before the day and agree a pick up time and place, as phone signal can be poor with so many people at Ragley.
  • After Nightfire it’s a long walk on grass in the dark, so I think a torch and sturdy shoes are a must.
  • The car parks all look the same after dark. I always drop a pin on my phone’s map when I park.
  • There was nowhere to charge phones in 2026, so I would bring a power bank.
