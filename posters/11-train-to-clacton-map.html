Train to Clacton

Clacton Airshow · Thursday 26 and Friday 27 August 2027

Get off at

Clacton-on-Sea, right at the end of the Sunshine Coast line.

How often

Every hour from London, with extra trains usually added on airshow days.

Then walk

Down through the town centre to the seafront, then turn right for the West Greensward.

Going home

Allow about 15 minutes back to the station. Trains are very busy straight after the flying.

Last train

The last direct train to London leaves at 22:05 in the current timetable.

Access

Step free to all platforms at Clacton-on-Sea.
