Train to CosfordSunday 13 June 2027

Your day
by train

08:00

Gates open

The showground opens. Parking and the station are both busy from early on.

08:25

Leave Birmingham New Street

West Midlands Railway. One train an hour stops at Cosford on a normal Sunday.

09:02

Arrive at Cosford

About 300 metres from the platform to the show. Follow the staff and signs.

09:15

Save your pitch

Head to the crowd line first, then look round the static park.

17:00

Flying usually ends

The 17:00 to 18:30 trains are the busiest, and the station runs a queue system.

18:30

Catch a later train

Look round the stalls, get some food and let the first rush go.
