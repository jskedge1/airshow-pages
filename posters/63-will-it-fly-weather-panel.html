Pilots can fly in rain. What they look at is how high the cloud is and how far they can see.
A showerOne that passes through in twenty minutes barely changes anything.
Clear skyWith lots of clear sky above them you get the full show, with the loops.
Lower cloudThe teams fly a rolling show, with rolls and wingovers kept under the cloud.
Low and murkyA steady layer of low grey cloud can keep the fast jets on the ground.
WindThe very old and lightweight aircraft need almost calm conditions.
