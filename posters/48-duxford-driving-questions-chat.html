Driving to DuxfordAir show weekends

You asked.
I answered.

Should I follow my sat nav?
Only until you’re close. Once you leave the M11 at junction 10, follow the event signs and the marshals.
Do I need to book parking?
Yes, for the Summer and Battle of Britain Air Shows. Every car needs a permit, booked with your ticket.
Will there be traffic?
Yes, on the big days. It builds on the M11 slip roads and the A505 from about 08:00, so arriving around 07:00 makes a real difference.
How do I get home easily?
Leave before the flying ends, or stay on and let the rush leave. The site stays open until 19:00.
