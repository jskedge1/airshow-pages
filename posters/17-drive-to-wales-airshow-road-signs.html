Drive to Swansea

Saturday 24 and Sunday 25 July 2027 · Park and Ride sat nav SA1 8LD

JUNCTION42

From the east

From Cardiff, Bristol and London. Leave at junction 42, Swansea East, and follow the signs for Swansea.

2
A483Fabian Way

Into Swansea

Follow the A483 towards Swansea for about four miles. Expect it to be very busy on airshow weekend.

3
P+R

Fabian Way Park and Ride

On the east side of the city. Pass under the footbridge, then take the next right. Buses run to the Civic Centre.

4
M4Eastbound
JUNCTION47

From the west

From Carmarthen and west Wales. Leave at junction 47, Penllergaer, for the A483 into Swansea.

5

Seafront closed

Oystermouth Road and Mumbles Road close along the seafront for the whole weekend, so you cannot drive up to the show.

Park and Ride was £10 a car in 2026. Closures are from the 2026 show, so check again once the 2027 plan is out.
