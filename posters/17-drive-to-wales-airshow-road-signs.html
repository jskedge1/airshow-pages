Saturday 24 and Sunday 25 July 2027 · Park and Ride sat nav SA1 8LD

Seafront closed

Oystermouth Road and Mumbles Road close along the seafront for the whole weekend, so you cannot drive up to the show.

2
P+R

Fabian Way Park and Ride

The way in I would use. In 2026 it was £10 a car, with directions emailed two weeks before if you booked.

3

Bus to the Civic Centre

In 2026 the buses ran 09:00 to 22:30 on the Saturday, for the evening show, and 08:30 to 20:00 on the Sunday.

4
P

Event car parks

Book before you set off. In 2026 they were £10 to £25, opened at 08:30 and had no re-admittance once you left.

5

Airshow

Free to watch from the seafront, from the Civic Centre to Brynmill Lane. Follow the official signs and stewards.

Park and Ride, car parks and closures are from the 2026 show. Check again once the 2027 plan is published.
