RAF Cosford Air ShowSunday 13 June 2027

Ways in
to Cosford

My advice

The train if you can manage steps, or arrive by car as the gates open at 08:00.

TrainMY PICK

300 metres to the show

Cosford station is right beside the airfield. One train an hour, and no step free access.

Car

M54 junction 3, then A41

Parking on the airfield is included in your ticket. Follow the yellow air show signs.

Drop off

Not at the airfield

In 2026 the nearest drop off point was the BP garage on the A41.

Bus

None on a Sunday

The show is on a Sunday, so the train is the only public transport option on the day.
