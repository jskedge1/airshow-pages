Your station for the showSunday 13 June 2027
Get off hereCosfordfor the Royal Air Force Air Show
1

On the train

One train an hour each way on a normal Sunday, so plan this train first.

2

Get off at Cosford

A small station right beside the airfield. No step free access to the platforms.

3

Then walk

About 300 metres, 5 to 10 minutes. There are staff and signs on show day.

4

Going home

Allow about 10 minutes back, and a lot longer after the flying. The station runs a queue system.
