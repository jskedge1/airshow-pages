Train route map · Cambridge and the free shuttle

Train to Duxford

Summer Air Show 19 and 20 June · Battle of Britain Air Show 11 and 12 September 2027

Get off at

Cambridge. The free Air Show shuttle bus leaves from right outside.

How often

Several trains an hour from London, from about 50 minutes.

The shuttle

Stand 5 at the bus interchange on Station Place. About 30 minutes to IWM Duxford.

Going home

In 2026 the buses back ran every 30 minutes from 15:30, with the last at 18:30.

From further away

Greater Anglia from Norwich, Ipswich and Stansted Airport. CrossCountry from Birmingham.

Access

Step free to all platforms at Cambridge.
