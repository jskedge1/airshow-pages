Bringing a camera?

Camera bag top ten

The short list for photographers. The full kit guide is in the Photography Hub.

One or two bodies
Two saves lens changes in the dust. One is fine for most people
A long lens for the flying
Something in the 300mm to 600mm range
A wide lens for the static
The one that stays on the second body
Spare batteries and cards
More than you think. Cold and long days eat batteries
Lens cloths and a blower
Dust, rain and fingerprints all day
A monopod, not a tripod
Tripods take room on the crowd line and some shows limit them
A bag you can carry all day
Sling or backpack
The sun guide for the show
Know where the light is before you pick your pitch
A small step or stool
Gets you over heads if you are not on the front row
Rain cover for the camera
Shows still fly in drizzle

Pack the night before and leave the bags by the door.
