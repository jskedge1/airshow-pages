What to expect when the weather is poor
Read the cloud.
Rain rarely cancels a show, but cloud changes the flying.
Low cloud does not
stop the flying.
It brings it down
to where you
can see it.
Full show
Cloud above 5,500 ft
Loops, vertical breaks and the big formation shapes.
This is the day everyone hopes for.
These heights are the ones the Red Arrows use,
and most display teams work in a similar way.
Rolling show
Cloud from 2,500 to 5,500 ft
The loops come out and wingovers
and rolls go in. Still a full length
display, just a lower one.
Flat show
Cloud from 1,000 to 2,500 ft
Fast passes, crossovers and
formation changes along the
display line. Loud, close and
great for photos.
Cloud base
The height above the airfield of the lowest cloud
covering more than half of the sky.
On the day
I would check the show's own social pages from about 09:00.
That is where changes to the programme are posted first.
