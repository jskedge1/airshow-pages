Camping weekend?

Camping top ten

For shows with camping on site or nearby, including RIAT, Duxford and the Midlands Air Festival.

Tent, pegs and a mallet
Hard ground in a dry summer, soft in a wet one. Bring both kinds of peg
Sleeping mat and warm bag
Summer nights in a field are colder than you think
Head torch
Hands free for the walk to the toilet block
Stove, fuel and a kettle
Breakfast and a brew before the queue for the gate
Cool box
Milk, lunch and the evening meal for two days
Camp chairs
They double as your crowd line chairs
Ear plugs
Campsites near airfields are noisy. So are campers
Wet wipes and a towel
Showers are limited or a long walk away
Bin bags
Rubbish, wet clothes and muddy boots
Power bank or a car charger
No sockets in a field

Pack the night before and leave the bags by the door.
