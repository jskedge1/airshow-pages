The gate,
step by step

1
Pick your laneStaff often split the queue into bag and no bag lanes, with a separate lane for wheelchairs, scooters and buggies.
2
Open your bagUnzip the main pocket and pull anything bulky to the top before you reach the table.
3
Scanner or hand searchYour bag goes through an X-ray scanner or a steward looks through it. Chairs, cool boxes and trolleys are checked too.
4
Arch or hand wandAt some gates, mostly military bases. You can ask for a member of staff of the same sex to search you.
5
Ticket scanAt the Air Tattoo the ticket check comes after the bag search. Phone brightness up.
6
Save your pitchHead to the crowd line first, then look round the static park and stalls.
