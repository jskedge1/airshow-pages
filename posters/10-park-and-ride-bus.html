Park
and ride

Park in a field. Take the bus to the gate. Loop all day at the bigger shows.
SHOWGROUND

The good points

  • No long walk with your chairs and bags
  • You avoid the slow crawl out of the car parks
  • Cheaper than premium parking at some shows
  • Buses can use roads that are closed to cars
  • Good with children, buggies or tired legs

Not so good

  • A queue for the bus after the finale
  • You are tied to the timetable and the last bus
  • Chairs and cool boxes come on the bus with you
  • No nipping back to the car for a jacket
  • The field can be as muddy as any other
Works well when you are happy to stay after the flying ends, let the first rush go and catch a later bus.
Park and ride for every featured show at ukairshowguide.co.uk. Independent visitor guide. Sites and prices change each year, so check the official show website.
