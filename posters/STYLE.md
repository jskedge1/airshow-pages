# Poster style rules

Rules for every UK Airshow Guide poster, from feedback on the Headcorn 2027 Thunderbolt poster.

## No "AI look"
- No coloured pill or tag boxes for headlines. Use plain text on the background, with a solid rule if a line is needed.
- No gradient strips, gradient lines or frosted, see-through panels.
- No rounded "card" boxes with soft borders.
- No icons in circles.
- No tilted badges or drop shadows. A badge is a flat block of solid colour with square corners.
- No coloured label text above facts (for example green "Show weekend" labels). Put the big fact first and a plain white line under it.
- No decorative dots or slashes between words. Use a comma.
- No white rule lines or dividing lines anywhere. Separate things with space and colour.
- Info at the bottom is three plain columns: a big amber italic date or place, with a white line of text under it.

## Keep
- Site colours: navy #13293C, sky blue #00A6E2, red #D74949, amber #F2B233.
- Barlow and Barlow Condensed fonts.
- Big uppercase italic title (it matches the slanted logo) with the second line in sky blue, and a red subheading.
- A flat amber badge, slanted to match the title, for the one key fact.
- A motif taken from the aircraft itself. On the Thunderbolt poster this is the red and white chequer band above the footer, copied from Nellie B's nose.
- White footer with the UK Airshow Guide logo.
- Jack's own photos only, with no photo credits on the image.
- No dashes in sentences, labels in sentence case, and advisory phrasing ("I would advise").
