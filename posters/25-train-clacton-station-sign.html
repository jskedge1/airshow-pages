Your station for the showThursday 26 and Friday 27 August 2027
Get off hereClacton-on-Seafor the Clacton Airshow
1

On the train

Direct from London Liverpool Street every hour. Change at Colchester or Shenfield from elsewhere.

2

Get off at Clacton-on-Sea

Right at the end of the line, so you cannot miss your stop. Step free to all platforms.

3

Then walk

10 to 15 minutes. Down through the town centre to the seafront, then right for the West Greensward.

4

Going home

Allow about 15 minutes back up to the station, and a bit more when the crowds leave.
