Duxford Air Shows19 and 20 June · 11 and 12 September 2027

Ways in
to Duxford

My advice

Book your parking permit with your ticket, or take the train to Cambridge and the free shuttle.

TrainMY PICK

Cambridge, then the shuttle

Several trains an hour from London. The free shuttle leaves from stand 5 outside the station.

Car

M11 junction 10

Every car needs a parking permit at the two big Air Shows. Car parks open at 07:00.

Park and Ride

Trumpington

The free Air Show shuttle bus also runs from Trumpington Park and Ride.

Going home

Let the rush leave

It is common to queue back onto the M11. The site stays open until 19:00.
