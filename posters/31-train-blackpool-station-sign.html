On the train

About four trains an hour from Preston. About 1 hour 20 minutes from Manchester.

Get off at Blackpool North

Then walk down Talbot Road to Talbot Square and North Pier.

3

Then walk

About 15 minutes. South along the promenade to the Tower, or a bit more in the crowds.

4

Going home

Head back up Talbot Road. After the last display the station fills quickly, so allow longer.
