By road · Little Gransden Air and Car Show

Drive to Gransden

Sunday 29 August 2027 · Sat nav SG19 3BP, then follow the signs
1
A428

Main routes in

The A428, the A1 and the A1198. Check for road works on the A428 and A1 before you set off.

2
B1046

Off the A1198

The airfield is just off the B1046, in the countryside between St Neots and Cambridge.

3

Air show

The last few miles are rural lanes. Follow the show signs and marshals, as sat navs can send you down the wrong lane.

4
P

Park on site

On grass fields beside the airfield, with marshals guiding you in. Gates opened at 10:00 in 2026, so arrive about an hour before.

5

Going home

Everyone leaves along the same narrow lanes. Leave a little before the end, or stay on and let the rush clear.

Nearest stations are St Neots and Sandy, both a taxi ride away. Check parking details when 2027 tickets go on sale.
