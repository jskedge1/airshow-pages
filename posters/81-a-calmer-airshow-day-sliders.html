A calmer
airshow day

The show you pick makes the biggest difference. With good ear protection and a plan, a calm day out is very possible.
Vintage and grass airfield showsOlder aircraft growl rather than roar. Fast jets are rare at Shuttleworth.
Smaller airfield showsMore relaxed, with room to sit back from the fence.
Seafront showsMiles of seafront with no gates, so you can walk away from the busy middle.
Big military showsRIAT, Cosford and the big Duxford days are the loudest and busiest of the lot.
GentlerLouder and busier
Towards the end of the crowd line it thins out and there is room to breathe. RIAT and Duxford both list a quiet space.
