Train to Cosford

The air show with its own station, right beside the airfield.

Walk

About 5 to 10 minutes from the platform to the show.

Trains

Every hour each way, with extra trains on show day in 2026.

Operator

West Midlands Railway. Some Transport for Wales trains call too.

Going home

Flying usually ends about 17:00. The 17:00 to 18:30 trains are the busiest.

Access

No step free access to the platforms, so check before you book.

Tip

Screenshot your last train home in the morning.
