Train to Cosford

Royal Air Force Air Show · Sunday 13 June 2027

Get off at

Cosford, a small station right beside the airfield.

How often

One train an hour each way on a normal Sunday, so plan this train first.

Then walk

About 5 to 10 minutes. On show day there are staff and signs.

Going home

Allow about 10 minutes back, and a lot longer after the flying. The station runs a queue system.

Extra trains

In past years West Midlands Railway has run extra trains after the show.

Access

No step free access to the platforms at Cosford, so check before you book.
