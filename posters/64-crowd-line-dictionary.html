crowd
line
/kraʊd laɪn/noun
1

The rope or fence that runs along the length of the display, with the runway and the display line beyond it.“Every ticket gets you to the crowd line. The front row is first come, first served.”

2

At a seafront show, the promenade, with the display over the water.“The beach itself is often the best spot.”

3

Where you save your pitch, with your chairs and a blanket, before you do anything else.
