Pack in
three layers

Top: what they need to seeCamera and lenses, power banks, spare batteries, medicines and anything with a blade, like scissors in a first aid kit.
Middle: food and drinkSandwiches and snacks in see-through boxes or bags, and sealed bottles stood upright.
Bottom: clothesWaterproofs, a spare jumper, hats and a blanket. These rarely need checking, so they can stay packed.
One bag is quicker than several small ones, and a wide top zip beats a drawstring sack.
