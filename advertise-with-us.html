Who reads the guides

People come to us before the big day, when they are still choosing a show, booking somewhere to stay and working out how to get there. That planning stage is exactly when a nearby hotel, a campsite or a good camera shop is most useful to them.

Our guides are written from first hand airshow experience, local knowledge and official information, so readers trust them and come back as their plans take shape.

We are building our audience for the 2027 season. We will share real figures as they come in, and we will never promise numbers we cannot show you.

Questions businesses ask

      
Cannot see your question here? Email us and we will happily talk it through.

      Ask us a question
    

    

      
Can I advertise on just one airshow guide?
Yes. The Business Listing and Featured Partner packages each cover one guide, so you only pay for the show your customers are visiting.

      
Can I appear on several shows?
Yes. The Regional Partner package covers up to three guides, and the Main Website Partner package is built around the shows and sections that suit you.

      
Can I use my own photos and logo?
Yes, as long as you own them or have permission to use them. We will confirm the sizes we need before your advert is built.

      
Will advertising guarantee bookings or sales?
No. We provide the agreed advertising space for the season. We cannot guarantee visitors, clicks, enquiries, bookings or sales, and we will only share performance figures we can actually measure.

      
Does paying change your recommendations?
No. Our visitor advice is written independently. Adverts are always labelled and kept separate from our recommendations, and any affiliate links are clearly marked.

      
I am a trader. Can you show that I will be at a show?
Yes, once your attendance has been confirmed by you or the event organiser. Until then we will not list you as attending.

      
When does my advert go live?
Once your placement is agreed, your artwork approved and payment received. The 2027 season runs from 1 March to 31 October 2027.

      
Are you connected to the airshow organisers?
No. The Big Guide to UK Airshows is an independent visitor planning website. Advertising with us does not mean any airshow or event organiser endorses your business.
