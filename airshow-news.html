HOW THIS PAGE WORKS

Two kinds of update, so you get the full picture without hunting round a dozen websites.

Checked every morning

Each day I read the official websites and organiser news pages for every show on this page, and add any new announcements here in plain English.

Live from Facebook

The Facebook feeds come straight from each show’s official page, so a new post shows up here the moment they publish it.

Always linked to the source

Every story links back to where it was announced. If something looks different on the official site, the official site is right.

This will work well when you are planning ahead and want everything in one place. On show day itself, I would suggest opening the show’s Facebook feed, as that is where late changes to the flying programme usually appear first.

I summarise each story in my own words and link to the original announcement. Facebook feeds are shown using Facebook’s own page plugin and belong to each show.
