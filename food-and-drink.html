Special Meals

Airlines give special meals a little code, so I have done the same. If you or someone in your group has a dietary need, this is what I would expect to find.

VGML

Vegetarian And Vegan

Easy to find now at most shows. Pizza, veggie burgers and noodle boxes are a safe bet.

GFML

Gluten Free

Hit and miss. Some vans do it well, others not at all. I would bring your own to be safe.

ALRG

Allergies

Busy vans and shared grills make it hard to be sure. Please pack food you trust.

CHML

Little Ones

Some vans do smaller portions for £5 to £8. Bring baby food and formula with you.

Baggage Allowance

Most shows are happy for you to bring a picnic, and honestly it is one of the nicest ways to spend the day. Please see the rules for your show before you go though, as I have seen people turned back at the gate. And please don't bring in prohibited items like metal knives or gas canisters. A plastic knife does the job just as well.

a trolley full of kit, picnic and all

Hand Luggage

  • Sandwiches and picnic food
  • Snacks, fruit and cakes
  • Soft drinks and water
  • Flasks of tea or coffee
  • Small cool bags

Check In Desk

  • Alcohol
  • Large cool boxes and trolleys
  • Camping kettles
  • Bag size limits

Not Allowed

  • Metal knives
  • Gas canisters and stoves
  • Glass bottles and jars
  • Barbecues, even disposables
  • Open flames
  • Selling food without permission

Pre-Flight Check

Pilots run a checklist before every flight, and I do the same with my food bag the night before. Tap each item as you pack it. There is something oddly satisfying about seeing them all go green.

Choose Your Cabin

There is no wrong way to do it. Some days I go full economy with a flask and sandwiches, other days I treat myself. If you are going on your own, have a think first. Leaving your camera gear at your pitch is risky, and carrying it all to the food vans is a pain, so for solo visitors a packed lunch is always the easier choice. Our View

The food at a show is never the reason you go, but it is often what you remember. I would advise flying economy for lunch and first class for one little treat. You will spend less, queue less, and be right there at the fence when the next display starts.
