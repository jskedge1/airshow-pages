Before You Join

The questions I get asked most about this part of the site.

Does it cost anything?+

No. The account, the hub and the forum are all free. I want the guide to stay free for everyone who uses it.

Do I need an account to read the guides?+

Not at all. Every show page and planning guide is open to anyone. You only need an account to save shows, plan trips and post on the forum.

Is the forum just for photographers?+

No. There's a Photographer's Corner for the camera crowd, but plenty of groups are about families, camping, getting there and first visits. If you like airshows, you're welcome.

See how the forum works

What's the difference between the hub and the Volunteer Hub?+

My Airshow Hub is for everyone. It's your own planner. The Volunteer Hub is only for people on the team, so they can send in photos, write ups and videos for the guide.

How to join the team

I run a stall or a business. Where do I go?+

Take a look at Airshow Traders and Advertise With Us. Traders can list where visitors will find them, and local businesses near the shows can get in front of people planning their trip.

Advertise with us

Can I change my mind and leave?+

Of course. If you want your account or anything you've posted taken off, drop me a message and I'll sort it for you.
