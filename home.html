A Guide Written To Give You The Best Day Out

I wanted to help others have a great day out. I have read so many comments from people whose airshow turned into a horror story, sat in traffic for hours or missing the display they came to see. I kept wondering how I could turn those into “best day ever” ones, and that is when I had the idea for this guide.

A day out on my own needs very different planning to a trip with friends or the family, so I have written every page with that in mind.

Your Day, Hour By Hour

This is how I would plan a typical show day. Times change from show to show, so I would check the timeline on each show page.

  1. 06:00Final Checks Before You Leave

    A quick look at the official website and social pages for last minute changes, and the forecast. Pack the car the night before if you can.

  2. 07:00Arrive Before The Car Parks Open

    I get there an hour or more early. The roads in are the slowest part of the whole day.

  3. 08:00Gates Open

    Tickets ready on your phone with the brightness up makes the gate much quicker.

  4. 08:30Save Your Spot On The Crowd Line

    Chairs and blanket down first, then relax and enjoy the morning.

  5. 09:00Static Aircraft And Pilots

    Lovely while it is still quiet, and the best chance to talk to pilots and ground crew.

  6. 11:00Stalls, Food And Toilets

    Food queues are longest around lunchtime, so I eat early or late.

  7. 12:00Flying Display Begins

    Everything you need out of the bags before the flying starts.

  8. 15:00Headline Acts

    The big names, like the Red Arrows, often fly in the afternoon. Check the timetable on the day.

My Arrival Tip

If you would like a front row seat, I would head there first and save your pitch, then have a look around. The toilet queues are busy all day, so I would go when you get the chance. A quiet moment rarely comes.

RIAT Is Moving To MOD Boscombe Down

From 2027 the Royal International Air Tattoo leaves RAF Fairford for MOD Boscombe Down, near Amesbury in Wiltshire. The travel plan for the new airfield is not out yet, so I would hold off booking trains until it is.
