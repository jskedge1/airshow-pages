On the day

The same short routine works at almost every show. I would suggest thinking about how into aviation you are, then picking the show and the spot that suits you and your family.

  1. Want a front row seat? Head there first

    Save your pitch on the crowd line before anything else. Check when the car parks open and arrive about an hour before.

  2. Then look around

    Walk the static show, speak to the pilots, visit the trade stalls and check the food options while the aircraft are still on the ground.

  3. Visit the toilet before the flying starts

    If you are not waiting to see a particular display, use that slot as a toilet break and miss the queues.
