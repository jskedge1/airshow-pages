RIAT moves to MOD Boscombe Down from 2027
Train to Cheltenham Retired

This route was for RIAT at RAF Fairford

The Royal International Air Tattoo is moving to MOD Boscombe Down, near Amesbury in Wiltshire, for 16 to 18 July 2027. The Cheltenham Park and Ride ran to RAF Fairford, so this plan no longer works. I have taken this page down so nobody plans a journey to the wrong airfield.

What happens next

The organisers haven’t released the travel plan for Boscombe Down yet. Shuttle buses, Park and Ride and the best stations will all change, so I would hold off booking trains for now.

I will build new route pages for the new airfield in due course, as soon as the details come out. The RIAT page has everything that is confirmed so far.
