Please check before you travel

Airshows can change their routes, car parks and ways in from one year to the next, and sometimes on the day itself. What's here is how it worked in 2026. Before you set off, check the official Clacton Airshow website and then follow the signs and stewards on the day.

Don't aim for the seafront car parks

The seafront car parks and the main Martello car park are taken out of use for the airshow. This catches a lot of people out. Head for the West Road car park or the Park and Ride instead, and try to arrive early.

Mind the residents only streets

Collingwood Road, St Vincent Road, Nelson Road and Trafalgar Road are residents only parking for the show, and wardens do patrol them. I would advise not chancing it. Switch on the residents only layer to see them on the map.

Step By Step

1
A133

Bovills Roundabout, make your choice

This is the first roundabout as you reach Clacton, and it's decision time. For the Park and Ride, turn off here onto Progress Way and follow it round to Clacton Shopping Village. For the West Road car park, carry on down the A133 into town. Think about which suits your family before you get here, as the queue builds quickly.

2
A133 and B1027

St Johns Roundabout

The A133 meets the B1027 from St Osyth here. Public parking around the town is signed on the day, so start watching for the airshow signs from this point and follow them over your sat nav. The town gets very busy, and the signs know which car parks still have room.

3
West Road

The event car park

The main event car park is on West Road, opposite Clacton Golf Club, and it's run by the Rotary Club of Clacton-on-Sea for local good causes. In 2026 it was £10 for a car and £20 for vans and motorhomes, with no overnight parking. Use CO15 1AJ for your sat nav. From there it's a walk along to the seafront.

4
The seafront

Closed roads by the show

Marine Parade West and Pier Gap are closed to traffic for the airshow. That's the road right along the top of the West Greensward, where the airshow village sits. You can't drive along it or drop anyone off there, so plan to arrive on foot from the car park, the bus or the station.

5
Going home

Don't miss the last bus

In 2026 the Park and Ride buses back to the car park ran until 10pm on the Thursday, for the evening flying and fireworks, and until 6:30pm on the Friday. Please check the times for your day before you travel. If you came by train, I would suggest having a look at the trains home before the flying ends.
