Camping & hotels

Where to stay for RIAT27

A new official campsite right beside the airfield, and hotels with event rates across the area. Here’s what’s confirmed and how I’d plan around it.

Camping

The official campsite

RIAT has secured a campsite next to the airfield, big enough to take everyone who used the official Fairford site and the campsites around it.

1st

Rollover bookings

If you booked camping for RIAT26 and rolled it over, you get first pick of plots at the new site.

2nd

The waitlist

People on the camping waitlist are offered plots next.

3rd

General sale

Anything left goes on general sale. Dates and prices are still to come.

Booked an unofficial site near Fairford? If it was a non-refundable booking, you can claim a free standard plot at the official campsite. RIAT hasn’t said how to claim yet, so keep your booking confirmation somewhere safe.
Hotels

Hotels with event rates

Hotels from 2 star to 5 star around Boscombe Down have signed up with RIAT. A booking tool for ticket holders is due soon, and some hotels will run a free shuttle to the showground.

Next door

Amesbury

The closest town, just north of the airfield and right by the A303.

South

Salisbury

The biggest choice of rooms, plus the station for the planned rail shuttle.

East

Andover

Good value, on the A303 and with its own planned shuttle station.

Further out

Further afield

Southampton, Basingstoke, Warminster or Swindon, using the trains and shuttles.

Book something you can cancel. Rooms around Salisbury and Amesbury will go quickly once the transport plan is out. A free cancellation room now lets you switch to an event rate hotel, or one on a shuttle route, later.
