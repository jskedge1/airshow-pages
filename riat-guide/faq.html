Questions about Boscombe Down

Tickets and bookings

Is RIAT still happening in 2027?
Yes. RIAT27 runs from Friday 16 to Sunday 18 July 2027 at MOD Boscombe Down in Wiltshire, with Park & View days on Wednesday 14, Thursday 15 and Monday 19 July.
Will it ever go back to Fairford?
No. RIAT has called Boscombe Down its new, permanent home. The RIAT office stays in Fairford, but the show itself won’t return there.
Are my RIAT26 rollover tickets still valid?
Yes. Existing bookings, including RIAT26 tickets rolled over to 2027, stay valid for Boscombe Down.
Can I buy tickets on the day?
No. Tickets are advance only, and they’re on sale now through the official RIAT website.
Do children still go free?
Yes. Under 18s go free with a ticket holding adult. Friday Futures and FRIAT are both carrying on too.
Can I get a refund?
RIAT asks you to wait for the transport, Park & Ride, hotel and camping details first. If you still want a refund after that, use RIAT’s refund form with your original booking reference.
What happens to the parking I paid for at Fairford?
It’s refunded automatically to your card (£15 a car, £7.50 a motorbike) by the end of December 2026. If parking is offered at Boscombe Down, you’ll get priority booking.
I booked an enclosure or the grandstand. What now?
Packages and upgrades are paused while the new options are worked out. RIAT will contact you about switching or cancelling.

Getting there and staying over

How do I get there without a car?
RIAT plans free rail shuttles from Salisbury, Andover and Swindon stations, plus Park & Ride. You’ll need a general admission ticket to board. Routes and times aren’t out yet. See Getting there.
Is there official camping?
Yes, on a new site beside the airfield. Rollover bookings get first pick, then the waitlist, then general sale. See Camping and hotels.
What about accessible parking and viewing?
There are no Boscombe Down details yet. RIAT Customer Services can help with your own needs on 01285 713456.
When do we find out which aircraft are coming?
RIAT usually starts weekly line-up updates in February. The headline theme for 2027 is the Tiger Gathering.

First time at RIAT

What I’d tell a first timer

RIAT is one of the biggest military airshows in the world. The flying runs for hours, the showground is huge and you’ll be on your feet all day, so a bit of planning goes a long way.

Arrive

Get there early

Car parks open at 06:00. The roads get busy fast, and arriving early means a better spot on the crowd line before the flying starts.

Pace yourself

It’s a long day

Saturday and Sunday have seven hours plus of flying. See the static park in the morning, then settle in for the display.

Leave

Have a plan home

Everyone leaves at once. Stay for the last display and let the queues ease, or know your shuttle times before you set off.

What to pack

My RIAT bag

  • Your tickets, on your phone and printed
  • Sun cream, a hat and sunglasses, as there’s little shade on an airfield
  • A waterproof, because July in Wiltshire can go either way
  • Ear defenders for children, and earplugs for you
  • A folding chair or a blanket
  • Water, plus snacks to keep you going between queues
  • A power bank for your phone
  • Comfortable shoes, ready for a lot of walking
Photography

Bringing a camera?

A long lens (300 mm or more) is the usual choice for display flying, with a wider lens for the static park. Bring spare batteries and cards, as you’ll shoot more than you think.
