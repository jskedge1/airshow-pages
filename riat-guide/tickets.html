General admission

A general admission day ticket gets you onto the showground for the flying display, the static aircraft park, the Techno Zone, the RAF Experience, the shops and the food. It’s also the ticket you need before you can add an enclosure, book camping or ride the planned rail shuttles.

Pick the day, or days, you want. Friday has a four hour plus flying display; Saturday and Sunday have seven hours plus, and are the busiest days.

Price guide. RIAT26 day tickets at Fairford started at about £47 for an adult. Check RIAT for the RIAT27 price before you budget.

Which day should I go?

  • FridayShorter display, usually a little quieter. Good for a first visit or if you can take the day off.
  • SaturdayThe longest days, and the busiest. Arrive early for a front row spot.
  • SundaySame long display. Leaving is often the hardest part, so have your way home planned.
  • More than one dayLets you do the static park one day and the flying the next. Pair it with camping.

Park & View tickets

Park & View is a separate ticket for the days before and after the show, when aircraft arrive and leave. For RIAT27 the days are Wednesday 14, Thursday 15 and Monday 19 July. Enclosure locations at Boscombe Down are still to be revealed.

FRIAT

Friends of RIAT is the show’s membership. It’s a package with its own enclosure and extras, and it carries on at Boscombe Down. In 2026 adult packages ranged from about £335 to £425, depending on the tier.
