Travel plan to be announced

Getting to Boscombe Down

RIAT is still working on its transport plan for the new site. This page covers what’s confirmed and how the area works, and I’ll add routes, car parks and shuttle times as soon as they’re published.

Where it is

Finding the airfield

MOD Boscombe Down sits just south of Amesbury in Wiltshire, with Salisbury a few miles further south. The A303 and Stonehenge are a few miles to the north.

By car

Driving

Most visitors will come in on the A303 from the east or west, or up from Salisbury. RIAT says car parks open at 06:00 each show day. Which gates and car parks you use will depend on your ticket, so follow the event signs rather than your sat nav on the day.

To be announced

Car parking

There’s no parking to book yet. If you pre-paid parking for Fairford, it’s refunded automatically by the end of December 2026, and you’ll get priority booking if parking is offered at Boscombe Down.

To be announced

Park & Ride

RIAT is planning Park & Ride sites as well as the rail shuttles. Locations and times will come with the transport plan.

By train

Free rail shuttles, planned

RIAT says the new site has much better public transport links than Fairford, and it plans to run free shuttles from three stations. You’ll need a general admission ticket to board.

East

Handy from London and Hampshire, on the line out of Waterloo.

South Western Railway

Trains from London Waterloo, Basingstoke, Woking and Salisbury.

North

Swindon

The “Swindon Shuttle”, for anyone coming from the old Fairford side or down the Great Western Main Line. About an hour by road.

GWR

Trains from London Paddington, Reading, Bristol, Cardiff, Cheltenham and Gloucester.

Don’t buy Advance train tickets yet. Until the shuttle times are out you won’t know which train you need, and Advance fares can’t be refunded. Book once the plan is published, and check for engineering works the weekend before.
Coming from Fairford?

If you used to go to Fairford

Your old routes won’t help this time. Boscombe Down is a different county, with different roads and stations, and RIAT puts Fairford and Swindon at about an hour’s drive away.

RIAT will run a Swindon Shuttle. Nothing is confirmed from Fairford itself yet. The old Kemble, Cheltenham and Swindon bus guides from the Fairford years have been retired.
