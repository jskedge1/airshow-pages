Royal International Air Tattoo

MOD Boscombe Down, Wiltshire · 16 to 18 July 2027

After more than forty years at RAF Fairford, the Air Tattoo is moving to MOD Boscombe Down, between Amesbury and Salisbury. The Big Guide to RIAT is back, rebuilt from scratch for the new airfield. I’ll add every piece of the plan here as the organisers release it.

What we know about Boscombe Down

The move was announced on 4 November 2026. This is everything RIAT has confirmed, and I’ll keep it up to date.

Venue

A new, permanent home

The flying and the showground move to MOD Boscombe Down, a military test airfield just south of Amesbury, with the A303 and Stonehenge a few miles north. RIAT’s office stays in Fairford, but the show won’t go back there.

Tickets

Your tickets still stand

Bookings, including RIAT26 tickets rolled over to 2027, stay valid. Under 18s still go free with a ticket holding adult, and Friday Futures and FRIAT both carry on. Local resident discounts are due in early 2027.

The show

The same show you know

The flying display, the big static park and the chance to meet crews from around the world are all staying, along with the Techno Zone and the RAF Experience. The 2027 headline theme is the Tiger Gathering.

Camping

A campsite next to the airfield

RIAT has secured a campsite beside the airfield, big enough for everyone from the old Fairford sites. Rollover bookings get first pick.

Camping and hotels → Hotels

Hotels with event rates

Hotels from 2 to 5 star around Boscombe Down are signed up, with a booking tool for ticket holders on the way. Some will run a free shuttle.

Where to stay → Travel

Free rail shuttles planned

RIAT plans free shuttles from Salisbury, Andover and Swindon stations, plus Park & Ride. Routes and times aren’t out yet.

Still to come

Waiting on the organisers

A lot of the detail isn’t out yet. That’s normal for a move this big, so I’d hold off booking anything you can’t change until the transport plan is published.

  • Car parkingNo parking to book yet. RIAT says car parks will open at 06:00 each show day.
  • Park & Ride and rail shuttlesPlanned from Salisbury, Andover and Swindon. You’ll need a general admission ticket to board.
  • Gate and flying timesFriday is a four hour plus display, Saturday and Sunday seven hours plus. Exact times come nearer the show.
  • Showground layoutWhere the crowd line sits, and so where the sun will be through the day.
  • Enclosures and grandstandPackages and upgrades are paused while the new options are worked out.
  • Park & View enclosuresLocations to be revealed in the coming months.
  • Accessible parking and viewingNo Boscombe Down details yet. RIAT Customer Services is on 01285 713456.
  • Aircraft line-upWeekly updates usually start in February.
Good to know

If you’ve already booked

  • Thinking about a refund?RIAT asks you to wait for the transport, Park & Ride, hotel and camping details first. If you still want one, use the refund form with your original booking reference.
  • Pre-paid parkingRefunded automatically to your card (£15 a car, £7.50 a motorbike) by the end of December 2026, with priority booking if parking is offered at Boscombe Down.
  • Camping near Fairford?A non-refundable booking at an unofficial site lets you claim a free standard plot at the official campsite. How to claim is still to come.
  • Enclosure bookingsRIAT will contact you about your options to switch or cancel.
  • Coming from Fairford or Swindon?RIAT says it will run a Swindon Shuttle. Swindon and Fairford are about an hour’s drive from Boscombe Down.
My advice for now. If you’re travelling a long way, hotels around Salisbury and Amesbury will go fast once people start planning, so a room you can cancel for free is worth holding. Leave non-refundable things, like Advance train tickets, until the shuttle plans are out.
