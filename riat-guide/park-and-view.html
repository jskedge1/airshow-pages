What is Park & View?

Park & View enclosures are fields next to the airfield where you park your car and watch from beside it. You’re not on the showground, so there’s no static park, but you see the aircraft land, taxi and take off.

Nothing runs to a timetable. Aircraft arrive when their transit flights allow, and RIAT warns that a few may arrive or leave outside the Park & View days.

Park & View tickets

Park & View is a separate ticket from the show days, sold per day through RIAT. Prices and booking for RIAT27 will come with the enclosure locations.

What to bring

  • Chairs, and a windbreak or sunshade if allowed, as you’ll be there for hours
  • A full cool box, water and a flask, in case catering is limited
  • Binoculars and a notebook or spotting app for serials
  • An airband scanner, if you have one, to hear when aircraft are inbound
  • Sun cream, a hat and a waterproof
  • A power bank for your phone and camera batteries

Dogs aren’t allowed in RIAT’s Park & View enclosures, the showground or its car parks.

Spotter tips

  • Arrive early. The first arrivals can come in during the morning.
  • Follow RIAT’s social media and spotter forums for expected arrival times on the day.
  • Wind direction decides which way aircraft land, and so which enclosure gets the closer view.
  • Monday departures tend to be busiest in the morning, so don’t leave it too late.
