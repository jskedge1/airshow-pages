Pre-paid parking from Fairford

Any parking you bought for RIAT26 will be refunded automatically to the card you paid with (£15 a car, £7.50 a motorbike) by the end of December 2026. You don’t need to fill anything in.

If parking is offered at Boscombe Down, people who had pre-paid parking will get priority booking, so keep an eye on your email.

Arriving by car

  • The airfield is just south of Amesbury, close to the A303. Expect that road and the routes into Amesbury and Salisbury to be busy from early morning.
  • Follow the event signs and the traffic marshals, not your sat nav. Your route will depend on where your car park is.
  • Have your parking and admission tickets ready to show, on your phone and printed.
  • Take a photo of your car park row or a nearby sign. Big show car parks look the same at the end of a long day.
  • Car share if you can. Fewer cars means shorter queues for everyone.

Getting away at the end

At any big airshow, leaving is the slowest part of the day. Thousands of cars head out at once.

  • Stay for the final display and let the first rush go. Pack up slowly, get something to eat and leave later.
  • Fill up with fuel before the day, so you’re not hunting for a petrol station in the queues.
  • Keep water and snacks in the car for the wait.
  • Staying locally? A walk or shuttle back to your room beats sitting in traffic.

Park & Ride

RIAT plans Park & Ride sites for Boscombe Down, alongside free rail shuttles from Salisbury, Andover and Swindon. Locations and times will come with the transport plan. You’ll need a general admission ticket to ride.
