Getting there

Park and ride, parking, public transport and the checks that save your day

Getting to the show is where most of the stress of the day lives. Decide how you are travelling before you book anything else, and check the live picture before you set off.

Several of the bigger shows run a park and ride. You park in a field or car park some way from the showground, then a bus takes you to the gate and back again at the end. RIAT runs several park and ride sites around RAF Fairford, and other shows use it when parking near the site is limited.

You usually book it in advance with your ticket, and the buses run in a loop all day. On the way in it is quick, because everyone arrives spread over a couple of hours. On the way out it is the bus queue that decides how long you wait, because everyone leaves at once.

Park and ride buses run in a loop all day. The queue builds after the last display.

The good points

  • No long walk from a distant car park with your chairs and bags
  • You avoid the slow crawl out of the on site car parks
  • Cheaper than premium parking at some shows
  • Buses can use roads that are closed to cars
  • Good if you have children, buggies or anyone who finds walking hard

The not so good points

  • A queue for the bus after the finale, sometimes a long one
  • You are tied to the bus timetable and the last bus
  • Chairs, cool boxes and camera bags have to come on the bus with you
  • No nipping back to the car for a forgotten jacket
  • The park and ride field can be as muddy as any other
This will work well when you are happy to stay after the flying ends, let the first rush go and catch a later bus. If you want to leave the moment the last aircraft lands, parking on site may suit you better.

Most airfield shows park you in fields around the site, with a walk of anything from five minutes to half an hour to the crowd line. Check when the car parks open and arrive about an hour before. For Shuttleworth I would arrive around 08:00 rather than 09:00.

Parking is often pre booked and priced by how close it is. Premium parking gets you nearer the gate, and sometimes a faster exit lane. Standard parking is further out and cheaper. Accessible parking usually needs a Blue Badge, and some shows ask you to contact them before the day, so check the show page.

Seafront shows are different. There is rarely a show car park, so you are using the town's car parks, park and ride schemes and side streets. They fill early, and the promenade roads close, so plan to arrive mid morning at the latest.

Grass car parks. Note the row you are in and take a photo of something you will recognise.

Plan

Before you book

Read the parking section of the show page. It lists the car parks, prices, what3words and postcodes, and whether you need a pass on your windscreen.

Arrive

On arrival

Follow the marshals, not your sat nav. Note the car park and row, and take a photo of a landmark. Thousands of cars in a field look the same at 18:00.

Weather

Wet weather

Grass car parks get churned up in rain. Tractors tow cars out at the big shows, but it is slow. Keep your speed steady and do not stop on a slope.

Public transport is a great way to get to many shows. Seafront shows are often easier by train than by car, because the station is in the town and the roads are closed anyway. RAF Cosford has its own station right by the showground. Several other shows run shuttle buses from the nearest station.

Every featured show page has a detailed public transport section with the stations, the train companies, the bus routes and the operators, and where possible the times around the end of the show. Open the show page for the detail, because it changes every year.

Why it works

  • No car park queue at the end of the day
  • You can have a drink if you want one
  • Often cheaper than fuel and parking for one or two people
  • Stations in seaside towns are a short walk from the seafront

What to watch for

  • Engineering work and rail replacement buses on summer weekends
  • Packed trains after the finale, sometimes queued onto the platform
  • Carrying chairs and cool boxes all day
  • The last train home may be earlier than the flying finishes
I would advise checking the train operator's app the night before and again in the morning, and knowing the time of the last train home before you leave the house.

  1. Open Google Maps from the show page

    Each featured show page has a button that opens the car park or entrance in Google Maps. Open it before you leave and check live traffic.

  2. Check train disruption

    National Rail Enquiries or the operator's app. Look for engineering work, strikes and any cancellations on your line.

  3. Check road closures

    The official show website and the local council traffic page. Seafront shows close the promenade and nearby roads from early morning.

  4. Check bus diversions

    Local buses are often diverted or stop short on show days. The operator's website usually has a show day notice.

  5. Check the official page on the morning

    Any change to parking, timings or aircraft appears on the organiser's website and Facebook page first. Our airshow news page tracks the changes for you.

Book ahead

Taxis and private hire

Each show page lists local taxi firms with numbers, including wheelchair accessible private hire. Book the return the day before, and agree a pick up point away from the closed roads.

Ask

Drop off points

Some shows have a drop off zone near the gate for people who cannot manage the walk from the car park. It is usually signed, and marshals will direct you. Check the show page.

Check first

Accessible parking

Blue Badge parking is closer to the gate and limited. Some shows need you to contact them in advance with proof, others are first come first served. The show page says which.

Our view

For the big airfield shows, I would advise park and ride if you are happy to wait for the rush to pass, and on site parking if you want to be away quickly. For seafront shows, take the train if you can. Whatever you choose, open the show page, check the travel section and check again before you leave.
