# Video styles: what Claude can make for the UK Airshow Guide

A menu of the kinds of video that can be made for the website, YouTube, Facebook,
Instagram and TikTok. When you want a new video, pick a style from this list.
Claude asks which style (and which platform sizes) before making anything.

## Sizes for each place

| Where it goes | Shape | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Website (in a page) | 16:9 | 1920 x 1080 | MP4, with a small preview copy so pages load fast |
| Website background (hero loop) | 16:9 | 1920 x 1080 | Silent, 6 to 15 seconds, loops with no join |
| YouTube | 16:9 | 1920 x 1080 or 4K | Plus a thumbnail picture and a title/description |
| YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Reels | 9:16 | 1080 x 1920 | Under 60 seconds works best; captions on |
| Facebook and Instagram feed | 4:5 | 1080 x 1350 | Captions on, since most people watch with sound off |
| Square (any feed) | 1:1 | 1080 x 1080 | Works everywhere when you only want one file |

Any video can be made in several of these sizes at once.

## The styles

### Already made in this style

1. **Animated explainer**: flat graphics, icons and text that move to explain how something works,
   such as planning a visit, the Hub or the journey planner. Voice-over or captions.
2. **Cinematic trailer**: your airshow photos and clips cut to music with big title cards.
   Good for a show page or a YouTube channel trailer. (See "Trailers made" below.)
3. **Stop-motion / cut-out**: paper-style pieces that move in steps, as in the website,
   transport, traders and volunteer adverts.
4. **Route / map animation**: a line drawing the bus, train or road route to a show, with stops
   and times popping up (the bus route videos).
5. **Short vertical series**: quick 9:16 tips videos made in batches (the TikTok series and the
   100 social videos).

### New styles to try

6. **Kinetic typography**: just words, moving in time with music or a voice. Good for quotes,
   "5 things to pack" or announcements.
7. **Photo story (Ken Burns)**: slow pans and zooms across your photos with captions. Calm,
   good for show recaps and "a day at..." videos.
8. **Website walkthrough**: a tour of a real page with zooms, a pointer and labels, showing people
   how to use the calendar, map, kit calculator or Hub.
9. **Countdown teaser**: "30 days to go" style clips for each show, with the date, the key acts
   and the link to the guide.
10. **Top 5 / list video**: "Top 5 places to watch at Duxford", "5 mistakes at your first airshow".
    Numbered cards, fast pace, works well on Shorts and Reels.
11. **Infographic / numbers video**: animated charts and numbers, such as crowd sizes, ticket
    prices, weather or the busiest arrival times.
12. **3D flyover**: a simple 3D scene, such as an aircraft flying over a stylised airfield or a
    camera moving over a show map.
13. **Quiz / "guess the aircraft"**: silhouette or close-up, a countdown, then the answer.
    Gets comments on Facebook and TikTok.
14. **Weekly news bulletin**: "This week in UK airshows", with news cards, dates and ticket updates,
    from the weekly facts and news page.
15. **Checklist / "what's in my bag"**: items appear and tick off, from the kit calculator and
    what-to-pack pages.
16. **Quote and review cards**: visitor or volunteer quotes animated over photos.
17. **Audiogram**: a voice clip with a moving sound wave and captions, for podcast-style tips.
18. **Animated poster / GIF**: a poster with a little movement (for example clouds, a jet trail or
    blinking text), short enough to use as a GIF or a looping post.

### Extras for any style

- Voice-over (written for you, or your own recording)
- Music and sound effects made for the video
- Burned-in captions or a separate subtitle file
- Your logo, colours and fonts from the brand folder
- A YouTube thumbnail, title, description and hashtags

Things to know before posting:
- The countdown says **209 days to go**, which is right on 16 November 2026 only. Ask for a new
  render with today's number on the day you post it (it takes about 15 minutes).
- The Cosford 2027 date is still to be confirmed, and the video says so. The times (gates 08:00,
  flying from about 11:30) are from the 2026 show, and the video says that too.
- The Top 5 guide photos are the site's AI images, so each has a small "AI generated image" note.
- The walkthrough uses real captures of the calendar page from 16 November 2026 (46 shows confirmed).
- The source for each film (code, shot list, review notes) is in `films/`, so any of them can be
  changed and rendered again.
