Privacy Policy

What I collect, why I collect it, who else handles it and the rights you have.

1. The short version

I built this site to help people plan a good day out at a UK airshow, and I would like you to feel comfortable using it.

You can read every guide without giving me anything about yourself.

If you join as a member, post in the forum, use the planning tools, send me a message or list a business, I keep what you give me so those parts of the site work for you.

I do not sell your information. I do not use it for advertising. You can ask to see it, change it or delete it at any time by emailing contactus@ukairshowguide.co.uk.

The rest of this page explains it all in more detail.

2. Who I am

UK Airshow Guide is run by me, Jack Skedge, from Essex in England. I am the person responsible for your information, which the law calls the data controller.

Some volunteers help me with guides, photos and the forum. Where a volunteer helps me moderate or answer messages, they only see what they need for that job and they follow this policy.

The site has a sister site, The Big Guide to RIAT (www.thebigguidetoriat.co.uk). It has its own policies. Joining one site does not sign you up to the other.

3. What I collect and why

Browsing the site

When you visit, the website platform records basic technical details such as your IP address, browser, device type, the pages you look at and roughly where in the world you are. I use this to keep the site secure, fix problems and see which guides people find useful.

Messages and the contact form

If you contact me, I keep your name, email address and what you write so I can reply. Sending a message does not add you to any mailing list.

Member accounts

To join you give an email address and a password, and you can add a name, a profile photo and a short bio. Passwords are handled by the website platform. I never see them.

The forum and My Airshow Hub

If you take part, I keep what you post: threads, comments, photos, likes, follows, groups you join, badges you earn and the notifications the site sends you. Your profile name, photo and posts can be seen by other members, and some posts can be seen by visitors who are not logged in. Please think before you share anything personal in a public post.

Planning tools

The trip planner, show checklist, must see aircraft list, My Hangar, saved tools, budget tracker, packing lists, past shows and your settings are private to you. I only look at them if you ask me to help with a problem.

Show Day uploads and crowd reports

If you send a photo of a flying display page, the site checks it is for the right show and the right day. To do that it asks your phone for its location, but only when you press send. Your location is used for that one check. I keep the result, such as about 1 km from the crowd line, not your exact position.

Crowd reports about car parks, toilets and queues are linked to your account so the site can stop spam.

Photos you upload

When you upload a photo, the site reads the camera settings so it can show them, then saves a fresh, resized copy. That copy drops hidden details such as where the photo was taken.

Traders and partners

If you list a business, I keep your business name, contact details, the shows you attend, your description, logo and photos. Your listing is public once I have checked it. If paid plans are switched on, payments are taken by the website platform and its payment providers. I never see your full card details.

Volunteers, contributors and organisers

If you offer to help or send me content, I keep your contact details, what you have sent, how you would like to be credited and any permissions you have given me.

Social media

If you message or comment on my Facebook, Instagram, YouTube or TikTok pages, those platforms also handle your information under their own policies.

4. My legal reasons

The law asks me to say which reason I rely on each time.

Contract: running your member account, the forum, the planning tools and any trader plan you sign up to.

Legitimate interests: keeping the site safe, replying to messages, stopping spam and abuse, checking Show Day uploads and improving the guides. I only rely on this where it does not override your rights.

Consent: optional cookies, any newsletter, emails about things you have asked for, and publishing work you send me. You can take consent back at any time.

Legal obligation: keeping records I must keep, for example for tax, or dealing with illegal content under the Online Safety Act.

5. Who else handles your information

I use a small number of trusted services to run the site:

  • Wix, which hosts the website, member accounts, the forum database, forms, chat and payments.
  • GitHub, which hosts some of the page designs. Like any web host, it sees your IP address when a page loads.
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the Barlow typeface. Your browser asks Google for the font, so Google sees your IP address.
  • Open Meteo, which supplies weather forecasts. The site asks it for the weather at the airshow, not at your location.
  • YouTube and Facebook, when you choose to watch an embedded video or view an embedded Facebook feed.
  • My email provider, for replying to you.

I do not share your information with airshow organisers, traders or anyone else for their own marketing.

I would only pass information to the police or another authority where the law requires it, or where someone is at risk of serious harm.

6. Information outside the UK

Some of these services store information in other countries, including the United States and the European Union. Where that happens, they use the protections UK law requires, such as approved contract terms or the UK and US data bridge.

7. How long I keep things

  • Member accounts: until you close your account. Your information is then removed within 30 days, apart from anything I must keep by law.
  • Forum posts and photos: until you delete them or close your account. If you would like your posts removed too, just ask.
  • Messages to me: up to 2 years, then deleted, unless they are part of an ongoing arrangement.
  • Show Day uploads: shown on the board on the day only. Held photos are kept for up to 90 days so I can check them, then deleted.
  • Crowd reports: shown for 45 minutes and deleted after 30 days.
  • Trader records and payments: up to 6 years after you stop, because that is what tax rules expect.
  • Reports of illegal content: as long as needed to deal with them properly.

8. Children

The guides are written for families and anyone can read them.

Member accounts, the forum and uploads are for people aged 16 and over. If you are 16 or 17, please talk to a parent or carer before you join.

Meeting up with other members through the forum, such as sharing lifts or camping pitches, is for adults aged 18 and over only.

If I find out someone under 16 has made an account, I will close it and delete their information. If you think this has happened, please tell me.

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information I hold about you
  • ask me to correct anything that is wrong
  • ask me to delete your information
  • ask me to limit how I use it
  • object to how I use it, including any marketing
  • ask for your information in a format you can take elsewhere
  • take back any consent you have given

Email contactus@ukairshowguide.co.uk and I will reply within one month. It is free. I may ask you to confirm who you are first, so I do not hand your information to the wrong person.

You can also change or delete most of your profile, posts and planning tools yourself from your account.

10. Keeping it safe

Member areas are protected by logins, the planning tools are private by default, and only I have admin access. No website can promise to be completely secure, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

If something ever went wrong that put your information at risk, I would tell you and, where the law requires it, the Information Commissioner's Office.

11. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how I have handled your information, please tell me first and I will do my best to put it right.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK regulator for data protection, at ico.org.uk or by calling 0303 123 1113.

12. Changes

I will update this page as the site grows. If I make a big change that affects members, I will let you know on the site before it starts.

13. Contact

Cookie Policy

What the site saves on your device, why, and how to say no.

1. The short version

Cookies are small files a website saves on your phone, tablet or computer. Some are needed for the site to work. Some help me see which guides are useful. A few come from services I show on the page, such as Facebook and YouTube.

I do not use advertising cookies and I do not follow you around the internet.

This page explains each type and how you can turn them off. It goes with my Privacy Policy.

2. What else counts as a cookie

The same rules cover similar tools, such as your browser's local storage, which saves small notes on your own device. On this page I call them all cookies to keep things simple.

3. Cookies the site needs

These keep the site running and safe. They let you log in and stay logged in, protect forms from spam, balance traffic and remember your cookie choices.

The site cannot work properly without them, so the law does not ask me to get your permission first. If you block them in your browser, logging in and some tools will stop working.

4. Remembering your choices

The site saves a few small notes on your own device so it remembers how you like things. For example:

  • whether you chose the Clean or HUD look for the forum
  • whether the hub sounds are on or off
  • which area you picked on the airshow finder
  • whether you have already seen the hub welcome screen or the add to home screen tip

These stay on your device. I cannot see them and they are not used to track you. The law allows these without permission because they only change how the site looks or works for you. You can clear them at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

5. Site statistics

The website platform counts visits so I can see which pages people read, how they found the site, what device they use and where pages are slow or broken.

I only use this to improve the site. The figures are added up into totals, I do not use them for advertising and I do not share them for anyone else's use.

Since February 2026, UK law allows statistics cookies like these without asking first, as long as I tell you about them and give you a free and simple way to say no. You can say no at any time using the cookie banner or cookie settings on the site, or by blocking cookies in your browser. Saying no will not stop you using any part of the site.

6. Cookies from other services

Some pages show content from other companies. They set their own cookies, which they control:

  • YouTube videos are shown in privacy enhanced mode, so YouTube should not set cookies until you press play.
  • Facebook feeds on show pages and the news page come from Meta. They do not load until you press the button to show them, so Facebook cannot set cookies through my pages unless you choose to see the feed.
  • Google Maps links and calendar buttons take you to Google, where Google's own cookies apply.
  • Payments, if you sign up to a paid plan, are handled by the website platform's payment providers.

Where the law says these need your permission, I will ask before they load. To find out what each company does, please read its own privacy and cookie policy.

7. Advertising

There are no advertising or tracking cookies on the site at the moment.

Some guides link to shops or booking sites. Where I might earn a small commission from a link, it is labelled. Clicking it takes you to that shop, and their cookies apply from then on.

If I ever add advertising cookies, they will stay off until you choose to switch them on.

8. How to control cookies

Use the cookie banner when it appears, or the cookie settings option on the site, to accept or refuse optional cookies. You can change your mind whenever you like.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Every browser lets you do this, and most have a private browsing mode too.

9. My sister site

The Big Guide to RIAT (www.thebigguidetoriat.co.uk) has its own cookies and settings. Choices you make here do not carry across.

10. Changes

I will update this page when I add or remove a service that uses cookies. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

11. Contact

If you have a question about cookies, email contactus@ukairshowguide.co.uk or use the Contact page.

Website Terms and Disclaimer

Using the site, checking details before you travel and what I am responsible for.

1. About the site

UK Airshow Guide is an independent planning guide for airshows across the UK, run by me, Jack Skedge. It covers dates, travel, parking, camping, accommodation, photography, accessibility and what to expect on the day.

By using the site you agree to these terms. If you join as a member, the Community Rules apply too. If you list a business or buy a plan, the Traders and Paid Plans Terms apply as well.

2. An unofficial guide

I am not the organiser of any airshow on this site and I am not part of the RAF, the Ministry of Defence or any event team. Unless a page clearly says otherwise, the site is not endorsed by the shows it covers.

Where I mention or show an airshow name, display team or logo, it is to help you find and plan for that event. Those names and logos belong to their owners, and I only show logos with permission.

3. Please check before you travel

Airshows are live events and things change, sometimes on the day. Dates, times, aircraft, car parks, road closures, buses, trains, prices, campsites, accessibility arrangements and event rules can all move after I write about them.

I check my guides and update them when I hear of changes, and some details such as line ups and show dates update automatically from official sources. Even so, a page can be out of date.

Everything in my guides is advice from my own visits and research, not instructions you have to follow. Please choose what suits you and your family, and always follow the official show rules and any directions from staff, marshals and police on the day.

I would always advise checking the official airshow website, and your train, bus, campsite or hotel, before you set off. If the official information differs from mine, go with theirs.

4. Aircraft, displays and weather

An aircraft listed on this site is not a promise that it will fly or be on show. Aircraft drop out for weather, serviceability and operational reasons, and organisers change running orders.

Weather panels, sun position guides and flight briefings are for planning only. Check a current forecast on the day.

Live tools such as Show Day, crowd reports and the arrivals board rely on members and outside data. They can be late, wrong or missing, so treat them as a helping hand, not an official source.

5. Your safety

Please stay on public or authorised land, follow signs, barriers and marshals, park legally and never block roads, gates or emergency access. No photo is worth going somewhere you should not.

Think about hearing protection for children, sun, rain, food, water and medication. You know your own family and needs best.

6. Accessibility information

I try hard to get accessibility details right, including Blue Badge parking, step free routes and whether you need to apply before the day. Arrangements change each year, so please confirm them with the show before you travel, especially if you need to send proof in advance.

7. Taxis, hotels, campsites and other businesses

I list taxi firms, accommodation, campsites, traders and local businesses to save you time. Each business is responsible for its own prices, bookings, refunds and service. Any booking you make is between you and them.

A listing is not a guarantee. Please check reviews and terms before you book.

8. Links and commission

The site links to many other websites. I do not control them and I am not responsible for what they say or do.

Some links to shops or booking sites may earn me a small commission if you buy something, at no extra cost to you. These links are labelled. I only recommend kit and places I think are genuinely useful, and a commission never decides what I recommend.

9. Member accounts

You need to be 16 or over to make an account. Please give a real email address, keep your password to yourself and tell me if you think someone else is using your account.

You can close your account at any time. I can suspend or close an account that breaks the Community Rules or puts other people at risk.

10. What you post

You keep ownership of what you post. By posting, you give me permission to show it on the site and in the site's own tools while it stays up. I will always ask you first before sharing anything of yours on the site's social media pages. You can delete your posts at any time.

Please only post photos and words that are yours, or that you have permission to share. The Community Rules explain what is and is not allowed.

All photos on this site are © Jack Skedge, unless another photographer's name is shown with the photo. In that case the copyright belongs to them and their photo is used with permission. The guides, maps, layouts, tools and designs on this site are protected by copyright too.

You are welcome to share a link to any page. Please do not copy guides, photos or tools to use elsewhere without asking me first. The Copyright and Contributors page explains how to ask, and what to do if you think something of yours is here without permission.

I work to keep the site running, but I cannot promise it will always be available or free of errors. Pages, tools and features may change, move or be removed as the site grows.

Please do not try to break, overload, scrape or get round the security of the site or its member areas.

Community Rules

How the forum, My Airshow Hub and Show Day stay friendly, useful and safe.

1. Why these rules exist

The forum and My Airshow Hub are here so people who love airshows can help each other: tips, photos, kit advice, car park updates and a friendly word for first timers.

These rules keep it that way. They apply to everything members post, upload, send or report on the site. By joining, you agree to them.

2. Who can join

You need to be 16 or over to have an account.

The show day buddy board, lift sharing, camping pitch sharing and any other arranged meet up are for adults aged 18 and over only.

3. Be a good airshow neighbour

  • Be kind, especially to beginners. Everyone started somewhere.
  • Disagree with the idea, not the person.
  • Keep posts about airshows, aviation, photography, camping, travel and the day out.
  • Give honest advice from your own experience.
  • Credit other people's ideas and photos.

4. What is not allowed

  • Anything illegal, including threats, harassment, hate, terrorism content, fraud, scams and anything that sexualises or endangers children. I report illegal content to the police or the right authority.
  • Bullying, abuse or targeting anyone, including pilots, display teams, organisers, staff or other members.
  • Content that encourages self harm, suicide or eating disorders.
  • Sexual or violent images.
  • Sharing someone's private details, such as their home address, phone number or car registration, without their permission.
  • Photos or videos of children that are not your own family, unless the parent or carer has agreed. Wide crowd shots are fine.
  • Other people's photos, videos or writing posted as your own, or without permission. This includes copying whole pages from airshow programmes or official websites.
  • Fake tickets, ticket touting above face value, counterfeit goods or anything sold in breach of an event's rules.
  • Encouraging people to trespass, enter restricted areas, fly drones where they are not allowed or break airfield rules.
  • Spam, repeated adverts, fake accounts, pretending to be someone else, or fake reviews.
  • Selling or advertising without a trader listing, outside the kit for sale or swap board.

5. Show Day uploads and crowd reports

  • Only send a photo of the flying display page when you are at the show, on the day.
  • Crowd reports about car parks, toilets and queues should be what you can see yourself, right now.
  • Never post anything that could compromise safety or security, such as restricted area access, security procedures or the live position of military aircraft on the ground.

6. Meeting up with other members

The buddy board is a great way to share a lift or a pitch, but you are meeting someone you do not know. I would advise:

  • meet in a busy public place first, such as the main entrance or a food area
  • tell a friend or family member who you are meeting and where
  • keep your own transport home or a way to leave
  • never send money in advance for a lift or pitch
  • trust your gut and leave if something feels wrong

I do not check members' identities. Any arrangement you make is between you and the other person. If someone makes you uncomfortable, please report them.

7. How to report something

Every post, comment, photo and profile has a report button. You do not need to give your name to the person you report, and they will not be told who reported them.

You can also email contactus@ukairshowguide.co.uk with a link or a screenshot. You do not need to be a member to report something.

If someone is in immediate danger, call 999 first.

8. What happens next

Every report goes straight into my admin queue and I look at each one myself. I aim to remove clearly illegal content as soon as I see it, and to review other reports within 48 hours. Show Day photos flagged by two members come off the board straight away while I check them.

Depending on what I find, I may:

  • leave it up if it does not break the rules
  • edit or remove the content
  • send you a warning
  • pause your account for a while
  • close your account and block you from rejoining
  • report illegal content to the police or the right authority

I keep a short record of reports and what I did, so I can deal with repeat problems fairly.

9. If you disagree with a decision

If your content was removed or your account was restricted and you think I got it wrong, email contactus@ukairshowguide.co.uk within 28 days and tell me why. I will look at it again and reply within 14 days.

You can also complain if you reported something and you are unhappy with what I did, or if you think I am not following these rules or my legal duties. I take freedom of speech and your privacy seriously when I make these decisions.

10. How I keep the community safe

I have looked at the risks of illegal content on this site and written down what I do about them, as the Online Safety Act requires. That includes the age limits above, the report button, my admin queue, automatic checks on Show Day uploads, and my own moderation. I review this whenever I add a new feature and at least once a year.

11. Your content

You own what you post. The Website Terms explain the permission you give me to show it, and the Privacy Policy explains how I handle your information. You can delete your own posts and close your account at any time.

12. Changes

As the community grows I may update these rules. The date at the top shows when they last changed, and I will mention big changes in the forum.

13. Contact

  • Email: contactus@ukairshowguide.co.uk
