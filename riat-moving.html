The Big Guide to RIAT is moving

Now that RIAT is moving to MOD Boscombe Down, I’m merging The Big Guide to RIAT into my UK airshow website, The Big Guide to UK Airshows.

Thank you to everyone who has visited this site over the years. I really hope it helped you plan your days at RIAT. With the 2026 show cancelled and a brand new location for 2027, though, most of what’s here doesn’t help you any more.

My new RIAT guide for Boscombe Down will be on the new website, and I’ll build it up as the organisers release more details. You’ll find it there along with guides for airshows right across the UK.
