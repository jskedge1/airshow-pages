Settings by aircraft

All five starting points side by side. Tap a column and the camera's screens change to match, so you can see what yours should look like.

Settings are a starting point. The light changes all day, so take a look at your pictures after the first display and adjust from there. I would keep ISO at 1200 or below so your pictures stay clean, and I wouldn't push the shutter speed faster than the aircraft needs.

Please note: this is a 3D model I've made for the guide. The layout follows the real D500, but small details can differ, and menu names can change with firmware updates.
