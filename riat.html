New home for 2027

Royal International Air Tattoo

Moving to MOD Boscombe Down, 16 to 18 July 2027

After more than forty years at RAF Fairford, RIAT is moving to MOD Boscombe Down, near Amesbury and Salisbury in Wiltshire. RIAT27 will be the first Air Tattoo at its new home, and I’m rebuilding my full guide around it. Most of the visitor details, like parking, shuttles and camping, haven’t been announced yet, so for now this page sets out what we do know.

What we know so far

The move was announced on 4 November 2026. This is what’s been confirmed, and I’ll add to it as the organisers release more.

A new, permanent home

The flying and the showground move to MOD Boscombe Down, a military test airfield just south of Amesbury, with the A303 and Stonehenge a few miles north. RIAT’s office stays in Fairford, but the show itself won’t be going back there.

Wiltshire

Your tickets still stand

Bookings, including RIAT26 tickets rolled over to 2027, stay valid for Boscombe Down. Under 18s still go free with a ticket holding adult, and Friday Futures and FRIAT are both carrying on.

Tickets

The same show you know

The flying display, the big static park and the chance to meet crews from around the world are all staying. So are the Techno Zone and the RAF Experience, with refreshed enclosures on the way.

At the show

A campsite next to the airfield

RIAT has secured a campsite beside the airfield, big enough for everyone from the official Fairford site and the campsites around it. Rollover bookings get first pick, then the waitlist, then general sale.

Camping

Hotels with event rates

Hotels from 2 star to 5 star around Boscombe Down have been signed up, with a booking tool for ticket holders due in the coming weeks. Some will run a free shuttle to the showground.

Where to stay

Better public transport

The organisers say the new site has better public transport links, and they plan to run free rail shuttles from Salisbury, Andover and Swindon stations, plus Park & Ride. No routes or times are out yet.

Getting there

Still to be announced

Quite a lot of the planning detail isn’t out yet. That’s normal for a move this big, and I would advise not booking anything you can’t change until the transport plan is published.

If you’ve already bookedGood to know
  • Thinking about a refund?RIAT asks you to wait for the transport, Park & Ride, hotel and camping details first. If you still want one, use the refund form with your original booking reference.
  • Pre-paid parkingThis will be refunded automatically to your card (£15 a car, £7.50 a motorbike) by the end of December 2026. If parking is offered at Boscombe Down, you’ll get priority booking.
  • Booked a campsite near Fairford?If it was a non-refundable booking at an unofficial site, you can claim a free standard plot at the official campsite. How to claim is still to come.
  • Enclosure bookingsYou’ll be contacted about the changes and your options to switch or cancel.
  • Shuttles from the Fairford areaRIAT says it will run a Swindon Shuttle to Boscombe Down. Nothing is confirmed from Fairford itself yet, and details will come with the transport plan.

My advice for now. If you’re travelling a long way, hotels round Salisbury and Amesbury will go fast once people start planning, so a room you can cancel for free might work well for you. Hold off on anything non-refundable, like Advance train tickets, until the shuttle plans are out.
