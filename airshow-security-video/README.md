## Steps
0. Intro: How airshow security works (base and ticketed shows)
1. Which shows search bags? Base and ticketed shows search bags; free seafront shows have no gate checks
2. Join the queue: bag and no-bag lanes
3. Get your bag ready: pack it in layers
4. What's in the bag? Usually fine, check first, not allowed
5. Into the search tent: X-ray scanner or hand search
6. Personal check: arch or hand wand at some gates
7. Ticket check: after the search tents
8. You're in! Head for the crowd line
9. Martyn's Law: expected from spring 2027
- End card: Pack light and arrive early!

## Sources behind the copy (checked 23 Nov 2026)
- The site's own guide: `airshow-security.html`.
- RIAT has searched every bag since 2023 (Gloucestershire Constabulary, after the Manchester Arena
  Inquiry). At RAF Fairford the ticket scan came after the bag search. For 2027 RIAT says it will
  have "airport-style security". Sources: airtattoo.com FAQs, the 2024 FRIAT forum PDF, and the
  Air Tattoo ticket and entry terms and conditions.
- Banned items:
  - RIAT bans weapons, pyrotechnics, drones and amplification devices.
  - BBQs or cooking are banned at RIAT, Cosford, Shuttleworth and Eastbourne.
  - Duxford and Shuttleworth ban ladders. Gazebos are restricted at Cosford, Duxford and Shuttleworth.
- Martyn's Law is the Terrorism (Protection of Premises) Act 2025. It got Royal Assent on 3 April
  2025 and is regulated by the SIA. It has a standard tier (200 to 799 people) and an enhanced tier
  (800 or more). GOV.UK (17 July 2026) says it is "expected to come into force in spring 2027".
  Qualifying events include ticketed events of 800 or more. Areas with no entry checks are not
  in scope.
- No official show page mentions walk-through arches or hand wands. The video only says "at some
  gates", which matches the site's own guide.
