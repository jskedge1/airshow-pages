      Photos that work

      • Your stall from the front, with the name or sign showing, so people can spot you on the day
      • Close ups of what you sell. Food photos taken in daylight look best
      • I'd say landscape photos fit the card a bit better than portrait ones
      • Only use photos you took or have permission to use
      • Photos on your card cannot be clicked to enlarge, so I would make sure they read well small
