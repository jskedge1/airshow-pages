Before you go

Good to know

Traders can change their plans, so if you are travelling to see one stall in particular, I would advise checking their own page the day before.

When you arrive

Where to find the stalls

You'll find most shows put their traders in a village near the main entrance, with food spread along the crowd line. If you aren't waiting to see a particular display, it can be a good chance to get round the stalls while the queues are short.

At the stalls

Bring some cash

I would advise bringing some cash as well as your card. Signal on a busy airfield can drop out, and I have seen card machines struggle at the bigger shows. I would suggest visiting the toilets and grabbing your food before the main flying display starts, as you will miss the worst of the queues.

For traders
